## §1 — Candidate Law 10.1 (final, Knox-ratified text)
Law 10.1 — Correction impact is a governed, versioned assessment over distributed lineage and explicit uncertainty; it is not a correction authority.
Within OMNI's managed boundary, every consequential producer and consumer must publish the source-attributed lineage and exposure evidence required by a versioned, testable coverage contract. When a source, state, relation, capability, model, policy, artifact, identity linkage, or version is challenged, corrected, superseded, withdrawn, or found defective, OMNI creates a versioned impact assessment whose physical name and home remain pending deduplication against Accountability's existing consequence_assessment_event candidate.
The assessment pins its input snapshot, effective and recorded time, assessment-logic version, evidence sources, coverage state, instrumentation-health sources, actor, authority, and purpose. It combines: traversal of recorded lineage; versioned cohort reconstruction across time, deployment, operator, patient, capability, model, policy, and environment; direct source/event correlation and human or counterparty evidence; returned external evidence and explicit uncertainty where downstream use is unobservable.
It produces a permissioned, minimum-necessary correction-impact projection identifying confirmed and potentially affected objects, actors, recipients, decisions, actions, and obligations. Any affected-set classes are assessment semantics, not locked schema enums until C5.
Proposed disposition is computed from at least: semantic_dependency × exposure_depth × affected_owner × consequence × current_validity × reversibility × time × evidence_confidence × coverage_health.
The assessment may propose invalidation, recomputation, amendment, notification, clinical reassessment, financial reconciliation, recall, influence review, no action with proof, or inability to determine. Neither the assessment record nor its projection owns any underlying domain truth and neither may itself correct, retract, reopen, notify, recall, compensate, or clinically reassess.
Runtime Operations owns instrumentation-health observations. Each producer owns its emitted lineage and exposure evidence. The relevant contract and E&V validation profile define required publication coverage. Each owning authority validates the proposed impact and commits its own disposition. Accountability owns only the response obligations admitted from the resulting consequences or duties.
Outside OMNI's managed boundary, OMNI preserves the exact payload, version, recipient, purpose, authority, transport evidence, acknowledgment evidence, and anything returned by the counterparty. Downstream use remains unknown until evidenced.
Missing evidence yields a source-attributed known set plus an honest not-ruled-out or indeterminate uncertainty statement — never "no impact" by silence. Necessary safety containment, legally required action, or urgent notification may begin immediately while cohort narrowing continues. The system must neither delay urgent containment awaiting perfect certainty nor convert the widest conceivable affected set into permanent maximal action without proportionate evidence, authority, and review.
Status: candidate architecture (design-validation PASSED). Not a spine law, domain, schema, or new universal object.

## §2 — Assessment record ≠ projection
- **Versioned impact-assessment RECORD:** durable record of question asked · snapshot+sources examined · logic/version run · coverage+instrumentation evidence · conclusion · what remained unknown · who assessed · under whose authority/purpose. May be a specialization/mode of Accountability's candidate `consequence_assessment_event` — dedup at C5.
- **Current correction-impact PROJECTION:** permission-aware assembled view of latest known affected set · candidate dispositions · open owner validations · admitted obligations · unresolved external states · coverage+uncertainty. Commits nothing; owns no facts.
- Mirrors OMNI law: state on owning object; views are governed projections that commit nothing.

## §3 — Dual-use-but-asymmetric proof fabric (candidate; routed to watch list as survived candidate, NOT canon)
OMNI's proof fabric is dual-use but ASYMMETRIC. Forward = authorized context assembly + consequential action proof. Backward = correction-impact reconstruction via recorded lineage + cohort reconstruction + direct evidence + external receipts + explicit uncertainty (NOT mere graph inversion — the failed-monitor case has no reverse edge). Differentiation claim (honest): OMNI makes healthcare-native limits on impact traversal (minimum-necessary visibility, exposure≠causation, adoption≠view) first-class, contractually-testable operating requirements rather than optional application config; earned only when enforced under real operator/agent/cross-org use. Commercial applications (AI-recall substrate · cross-operator correction network · regulator-facing reconstruction · insurer risk reduction) are **pilot hypotheses**, not spine claims. The graph itself is generic; the differentiation is the healthcare-native governed composition + proven cross-authority operation (unproven).
