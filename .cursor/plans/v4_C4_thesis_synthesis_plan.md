# v4 — C4 Thesis Synthesis Plan (how the accepted spine becomes the fuller thesis)

Document type: `plan_or_roadmap` (C4 named gate #3) · Authority: `analysis_nonbinding` (`GRD-036`) — a method/gate plan; **NOT thesis prose.**
Status: `accepted` 2026-06-15 (Nick + Knox). C4 runway package. Runs AFTER the spine (docs #1/#2) is authored + accepted. The spine = the controlling skeleton (*what OMNI IS*, terse); the **thesis** = the fuller articulation (depth, mechanism, evidence, tensions, ownership-laws) per section.

## §0 Spine vs thesis (do not conflate)
- **Spine** = the accepted controlling doc: one-sentence thesis + section claims + non-goals + open questions. Terse, timeless, authority-true.
- **Thesis** = the full-body articulation of each accepted spine section — the mechanism, the depth, the reconciled evidence, the named tensions + resolutions, the ownership-laws — still timeless prose. **Synthesis does NOT re-open the spine's claims;** it deepens them. If synthesis surfaces a spine-level contradiction → route to the spine gate, do not silently rewrite.

## §1 Inputs per section (what feeds the deepening)
For each accepted spine section, the synthesis pulls (C4.0 Depth Packet discipline):
- the section's **worktable** (from the authoring pass) + its accepted claim;
- the **richest full-body source articulation** (registry §1 + per-source §3 Review-003 + ORIENTATION verbatim + v2/v3 body + the relevant arc artifacts via their use-maps) — **actively mined, not one-liners**;
- the **contracts** the section composes/pressures (P1 truth boundaries — cite, do not contradict);
- the **tension-register** entries for that section (carry the named tension + its resolution: settled-by-doctrine | v4-decision | open-review);
- the **ownership-law** for that section (who owns what; the "X owns truth, Y owns lifecycle-state" decompositions).

## §2 Method (per-section deepening loop)
1. Take the accepted spine claim (immutable here).
2. Pull the Depth Packet (richest articulation) — **fail if the section deepens from a one-liner / lens-name only (C4.0).**
3. Weave: claim → mechanism → why → ownership-law → reconciled evidence → named tension+resolution → forward pointer (contract-rewrite requirement / open question).
4. Adjudicate any new tension surfaced → register → route. No unrouted tensions (`GRD-043`).
5. Author **timeless** prose (no journey narrative; positioning kept).
6. Depth check: a reviewer can point to the mechanism/tension/ownership-law behind every lens NAME.
