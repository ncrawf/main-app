## I.1 Purpose, problem & non-goals

**Dual genesis (both problems are co-equal — neither swallows the other; Knox):**
- **(A) Source Estate.** Federation onboarding exposed a category error: agents treated a 50,000-file institutional corpus as *exhaust* on the way to normalized domain records. **What OMNI received must never evaporate into what OMNI concluded.** S1 exists as a first-class class precisely so received source/corpus custody is durable, independent, and re-interpretable — separate from any conclusion drawn from it.
- **(B) Knowledge / Foundry.** The old CNS/Knowledge-Reservoir work was provisional; the outside-learning waves + Agent-Runtime work fired the deferred `FWREG-007` revisit trigger. OMNI must **govern reusable knowledge, projections, context, maintenance, and continuous learning without re-deriving its own intelligence** from scattered chats/videos/packets (this very arc was an instance of that failure).

**Problem (unified).** Remember, reuse, and improve knowledge across patients, providers, operators, federations, vendors, and OMNI's own build — over years — without (a) flattening distinct kinds of knowledge or **collapsing received-source into concluded-truth**, (b) letting stored material silently become truth, or (c) leaking one principal's material to another.

**What this architecture is.** A governed **source→knowledge→context** substrate: durable source custody (S1), governed reusable-knowledge bodies (S2), owner-committed domain truth (S3), external-evidence interpretation through S4 plus broader extraction and normalization through R.6 machinery, regenerable projections/wikis (S5), and runtime context assembly (S6) — joined by an integrated evolution capability (the **OMNI Intelligence Foundry**) that proposes consequences into architecture, contracts, tests, evals, product doctrine, and Build OS, under review gates, with lineage and proof.

**What it is NOT.** Not a generic RAG/vector/memory app (retrieval is replaceable infrastructure, never truth); not one "everything store" (the god-category "Governed Context Estate" was rejected); not an EMR (patient truth is S3, owned by care domains); not a cross-tenant brain (no principal sees another's material by default); not Palantir's proprietary-ontology model (OMNI owns a *portable* healthcare semantic constitution, not a lock-in ontology).

| class | is | commits | is NOT |
|---|---|---|---|

| **S3 Domain-Owned State** | owner-committed domain/governance truth (Clinical Memory, care state, ops state, governance control-plane) | **domain + governance state** (the operative commitment) | not a reservoir; **not** the sole committer of *every* kind of truth (S1 owns receipt-truth; S2 owner accepts knowledge) |

**End-to-end lifecycle (a graph, not a straight line):** `acquire → preserve (S1, receipt committed) → interpret [S4 for external evidence · R.6 for general extraction] → propose → admit (S2 owner) OR adopt (S3 owner) → use (bounded by use_ceiling) → project (S5) → assemble (S6, run-receipt emitted) → act ONLY through the owning domain → observe outcome (S3) + run evidence (S1) → reconsider (on contradiction/invalidation) → possible new S2/S3 candidate`. **Storing is not adopting; indexing is not accepting; a retrieval hit authorizes nothing; and *normative force is never a property of the stored unit* — it is owned by the domain/governance state that adopts it.**

## I.3A Operational decision-and-override lineage seam (the two-speed law — controlling)

*Added as a G5 integration delta (bounded post-test patch, 2026-08-02) — the run-1 three-angle test proved this seam was under-specified in the packet: Angle A could not trace an operational override, and Angle C could source the two-speed law only from the prompt, not the packet. It is **not** a new class; it is the operative expression of the I.3 lifecycle's two clocks. Nothing here mints an "instant memory" class.*

**Law.** *Immediate authorized operational adaptation does NOT wait for S2 admission.* The owning domain or governance home commits the current operative state or configuration; an immutable S1 decision/run receipt records the event; the next authorized Context Router draw may consume that current owner-state immediately. **Reusable generalization is a separate candidate → review → S2 admission path.** One local override never silently becomes operator-wide, platform-common, or network knowledge.

**FAST clock (operational adaptation — must not depend on S2).** When an authorized actor takes or overrides an action that changes current behavior, the following are committed/recorded **at the time it occurs**:
1. **proposed action** (what the agent/actor proposed) — record;
2. **authority evaluation** (which authority/capability/role was checked, and its result);
3. **actual action / override** (what was actually done, and by whom);
4. **reason** (the override rationale) + **context & source references** (what the decision saw);
5. **policy / model / configuration versions** in force at decision time (pinned);
6. **effective time and recorded time** (bitemporal — when it takes effect vs when it was logged);
7. **scope + expiry / reconsideration trigger** (recorded across the four orthogonal scope dimensions below);
8. **owner commit:** the **owning domain / Settings / governance home** commits the current operative state or configuration (**S3 / Settings / owning governance** — never S2, never runtime working memory);
9. **immutable S1 decision/run receipt** of 1–8 (evidence, not truth-by-generation);
10. **immediate next authorized S6 draw** may consume that current owner-state at once (the fast clock does not block on the slow clock).

**The exact owner of a local constraint is the owning operational domain/governance home** (e.g. Scheduling domain state, or an operator Settings/policy home) — **not** the Foundry, **not** the Agent Runtime, **not** an S2 unit.

**Four ORTHOGONAL planning dimensions a downstream author must keep separate (Knox pre-flight type correction — the earlier "exactly one of six scope kinds" was ill-typed: it collapsed operational duration, owner-state kind, knowledge lifecycle, and promotion scope into one mutually-exclusive field).** These are **planning dimensions, NOT final schemas/enums** — C5 authors the concrete record shape. **One operational event occupies a value on EVERY dimension at once, and the same underlying event typically moves across values over time** (e.g. a room outage is a `time_bounded` `temporary_constraint` now; may later generate a `candidate` lesson; may later become an `adopted` local `policy`; may later — through a separate gate — reach `federation_promoted`):

1. **`operational_effect`** — how long the local effect is currently operative: `action_only` · `time_bounded` · `continuing`
2. **`owner_state_kind`** — what kind of owner-state was committed: `action_result` · `temporary_constraint` · `configuration` · `policy`
3. **`knowledge_lifecycle`** — where the reusable-learning candidate sits (independent of 1–2): `none` · `candidate` · `under_review` · `admitted` · `rejected` · `superseded`
4. **`promotion_scope`** — how far any adoption/promotion has reached: `none` · `principal_local` · `operator_local` · `federation_candidate` · `federation_promoted`

**Constitutional law: these four questions stay separate — they are not competing values in one field.** The **fast** owner-state path (dimensions 1–2) and the **slow** reusable-learning path (dimensions 3–4) remain independent; collapsing them re-collapses the very fast/slow distinction this seam exists to protect. This is how OMNI avoids turning every local workaround into universal policy while still learning quickly from real operations.

**SLOW clock (reusable generalization — the only path to S2).** `S1 action/override/outcome evidence → candidate extraction → recurrence + applicability-envelope assessment → S2 review/admission → optional owner-policy/configuration proposal → authorized adoption OR rejection → reconsideration`. An admitted S2 unit is still **not operative** until an owning S3/Settings state adopts it (I.4 `use_ceiling` + adoption-link law).

**Hard laws (falsifiers).**
- **S2 is never live owner-state.** If the fast clock writes an override into S2 to make the next run see it, the seam is violated.
- **Runtime working memory is never durable authority.** The override becomes durable only by an owning-domain/Settings commit + its S1 receipt — not by living in an agent's memory.
- **One local override never silently becomes operator-wide, platform-common, or network knowledge.** Cross-principal promotion requires the separate promotion gate that advances `promotion_scope` to `federation_promoted` (Foundry federation profile, I.6).
- **No "instant memory" class.** The behavior is fully represented by S3/Settings owner-state (fast) + S1 receipt (evidence) + S2 candidate/admission (slow). Minting a seventh class is rejected.

**Worked override trace (the fixture Angle A must be able to reconstruct from this packet).** Scheduling agent proposes slot X; clinic manager overrides to slot Y because a room is out of service. → proposal record (X) + authority eval (manager holds Scheduling override capability) + actual override (Y) + reason ("room 3 out of service") + versions (scheduler policy vN, model vM) + effective=now/recorded=now + **scope across the four dimensions** (`operational_effect=time_bounded` · `owner_state_kind=temporary_constraint`, expires when the room returns · `knowledge_lifecycle=none` · `promotion_scope=none`) → **Scheduling domain S3 commits the temporary local constraint**; **S1 records the immutable decision/run receipt** → the **next Context Router draw immediately sees the Y-constraint** (fast clock, no S2 wait). **Independently, on the slow clock:** once analytics accumulate **sufficient governed recurrence evidence under the applicable consequence-tier, admission-operations, and review policy** (no arbitrary threshold is canonized in G5 — the threshold belongs to C5 admission operations, Task-D operating economics, and Build-Entry proof design) the event's `knowledge_lifecycle` advances to `candidate` — an S2 candidate (`subject=operations · form=lesson · epistemic=observed · authority_class=institutional_record · admission_scope=operator_local · applicability=this clinic`) enters review (`under_review`) → if `admitted`, an **explicit S3/Settings adoption link** makes it operative (`owner_state_kind=policy` · `promotion_scope=operator_local`) **for that operator only** → outcome observed (S1/S3) → reconsideration. **Cross-tenant promotion (`promotion_scope=federation_promoted`) is blocked** unless the separate federation promotion gate fires. (This trace is the seam; the surrounding fan-out is I.2/I.3.)

## I.4 Knowledge Reservoir classification (corrected — orthogonal axes, not a roster)

S2 is **not** a set of branded reservoir databases. A reservoir member is a **typed reusable-knowledge unit** — a point in a **genuinely orthogonal** axis space (this follows accepted G2 §8.1 multidimensionality; it corrects the earlier flat "6/8 roster", the collapsed 3-axis draft, **and** the prior draft's non-orthogonal `source_family`/`source_scope`/`reality_mode`/`normative_posture` axes that combined multiple questions). Each axis below answers **one** distinct question:

| group | axis | cardinality | values |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Subject** | `primary_subject_domain` | exactly 1 | clinical · operations · product · build · risk · brand |
| | `secondary_domain_tags[]` | 0..n | any of the above (e.g. an ops lesson tagged `risk`) |
| **Content** | `primary_semantic_form` | exactly 1 — **controls lifecycle/review** | claim · pattern · procedure · model · synthesis · lesson |
| | `secondary_forms[]` | 0..n | a `synthesis` may also carry `claim`/`pattern`; a `lesson` may carry `pattern`/`procedure` |
| **Origin** | `origin_party` | 1 | first_party · third_party — **relative to the admitting principal/estate, not to OMNI universally** |
| | `admission_scope` | 1 | principal_local · operator_local · federation_shared · OMNI_internal · public_reference |
| | `access_policy_refs[]` | 0..n | **references to owning grant/permeability policies, resolved at request time** (NOT an intrinsic enum; see rules below) |
| | `reality_basis` | 1 | real_world · simulated |
| | `epistemic_mode` | 1 | observed · reported · inferred · synthesized |
| | `source_kind` | 1 primary + 0..n secondary | literature · guideline · case_report · incident_record · manual · policy · advisory · vulnerability_record · telemetry · simulation_run · model_output · … (the publication/record *kind*) |
| | `authority_class` | 1 primary + 0..n secondary | guideline_body · regulator · manufacturer · publisher_or_journal · expert_consensus · institutional_record · principal_assertion · patient_report · model_output · other_governed (the *originator/authority* basis — **never a publication kind, never a catch-all**) |
| **Authority** (retained G2) | | | `principal/tenant` · `authority_basis` · `review_authority` · `applicability/scope` · **`use_ceiling`** · `consequence_class` · `freshness` · `sensitivity` · `admission/activation/currency/materialization` states |

**`admission_scope` ≠ artifact custody (Knox §2).** `admission_scope` = **the governed principal/tenant/profile scope within which this reusable-knowledge unit was admitted and may be maintained.** It is **NOT** S1/D7 physical or legal artifact custody — that lives separately in S1 lineage/artifact relations. (`public_reference` is an admission scope, not a custody claim over the source bytes.)

**`access_policy_refs[]` — visibility is not intrinsic (Knox §3).** Who may read a unit is **evaluated at request time** against 0..n composed grant/permeability policies. Rules: **default-deny when no permission resolves**; **many grants/policies may compose**; **the knowledge unit never intrinsically grants access to itself** (one canonical unit, many grants). This is why visibility is a plural policy relationship, not a single baked-in enum.

**`source_kind` (publication/record kind) ≠ `authority_class` (originator/authority basis) (Knox §1).** `literature` is a **`source_kind`**, never an `authority_class`; a journal meta-analysis is `source_kind=literature · authority_class=publisher_or_journal`. A provider's personal preference is `authority_class=principal_assertion` (the provider need not be the operator). An operator/build/brand internal record is `authority_class=institutional_record`. **No `operator_record` universal catch-all.** Each of `source_kind`/`authority_class` carries **primary + secondary tags** where real cases are mixed.
