## §1 What OMNI is (re-grounded)

OMNI is a **governed contextual care + business operating substrate**. Its center of gravity is not an app, EMR, scheduler, CRM, telehealth platform, or marketplace — those are product surfaces. OMNI's job is the substrate physics underneath: identity, consent, authority, clinical memory, observation, documents, scheduling, service occurrence, commerce, messaging, fulfillment, federation, RBAC, AI lineage, CNS orchestration, audit — preserved as **longitudinal coherence over time**, so that *care remembers the patient* and operators coordinate without losing ownership or visibility boundaries.

**The v3 sharpening (the defining property):** that coherence must now hold **across every actor and every rail.** A patient may act through OMNI Direct, a brand app, a partner portal, a voice assistant, or their own external AI agent; a provider through a workspace or an agent; an operator through OMNI-operated or partner systems; and increasingly through **non-human actors** (agents, models, tools) that discover each other, request context, and invoke capabilities. Whatever the surface or rail, OMNI preserves **identity, authority, consent, context, proof, ownership, and commit boundaries underneath.** That is the substrate.

**Care is the business and the moat.** The owned care operations (OMNI-operated Specialty Lines + Core Capabilities + Powered-by-OMNI Brands) are where trust is created, patients originate, longitudinal context accrues, and revenue is made. The **trust axis** (§A) and the **AI axis** (§B) are cross-cutting properties that keep that care substrate coherent and safe — they are not the product and not the moat. The moat is **care-governance semantics**: what consent means clinically, what authority and adoption mean, longitudinal coherence, `care_commitment`, operator-boundary meaning, the candidate→commit boundary.

(Operating model = two interlocking governed loops (Sense + Act) + authority gates — inherited §8/§8.6, threaded by the axes in §B.2.)

## §2 What OMNI is not (re-grounded)

- **Not a fortress with bolted-on APIs** (the Mindbody failure: a silo the agent layer routes around and demotes to a dumb backend).
- **Not an AI / agent platform.** AI is an axis, not the target; the agentic-runtime / MCP / A2A / model-routing layer is commodity infrastructure (`D0THES-GRD-028`). Building *there* is suicide; the moat is care-governance.
- **Not a trust network that happens to do care.** A contentless trust/clearing network is a low-margin utility ("Plaid for healthcare" where care = seed, network = business) — a different, weaker company. Trust is a decomposed axis (§A), not the identity.
- **Not OMNI-Direct-the-destination.** OMNI Direct is **architecturally one rail among many.** (It is **not economically demoted** — see §A.6.)
- **Not "AI everything," "forms everything," or "event-bus everything"** (inherited v2 §9 rejected paradigms, preserved).
- Substrate-permissive ≠ product-permissive (inherited §2/§3 plane discipline).

## §A The Trust / Authority / Permeability axis (v3 NEW, first-class)

### §A.1 Why an axis, not a domain
The agentic world (agents, MCP/A2A, non-human actors discovering each other and requesting context) makes governed trust *more* necessary, not less — but it does not create a new domain. Trust was always decomposed (§3 four-way composition). v3 names it a **first-class axis** so it stops being implicit. **Federation owns the cross-boundary topology layer of the axis — bounded, never a god-domain** (`D0THES-GRD-031`).

### §A.2 Trust is relocated, not eliminated (the v39 doctrine)
Every outward rail (MCP gateway, vendor model, partner API, encrypted tunnel, capability server) is a **new trust concentrator.** A secure rail *transfers* trust; it does not remove it. Metadata, intent, actor, endpoint, and timing leak even when payload is encrypted; logs/traces can be compelled or monetized. **Therefore every outward rail must name its trust-transfer (who logs / who can be compelled / who monetizes metadata) and govern metadata, not just payload; "encrypted therefore safe" is rejected; topology (centralized fast-path vs compartmentalized high-risk) is chosen per sensitivity** (`D0THES-GRD-030`). Context-packet exchange carries a `trust_transfer_record`.

### §A.3 Non-human actors + delegated authority (net-new)
- **Non-human actor** (Identity): a registered agent with a unique instance ID, a **represented principal**, and a **delegation chain** — never lazy user-impersonation (the v41 anti-pattern). Distinct from the existing `device`/`robot`/`external_system` subtypes; only humans hold §7.2 care-ownership.
- **Delegated-authority / agentic-consent envelope** (RBAC + Federation): who delegated, what actions, scope, lifetime/TTL, purpose, revocation, just-in-time escalation; **per-transaction, transparent, revocable** (the v25/v29 "living contract … act with us, not instead of us"). RBAC's 4-tier attestation + CIBA/JIT for high-impact acts.
- **Short-lived, intent-bound credentials + the "agentic last mile"** (Federation/capability layer): authority must **survive to the action** — no standing shared backend keys; a vault/policy-enforcement-point re-binds *user + agent + intent + delegation* and issues per-action credentials, with a pre-action decision receipt.

### §A.4 Inbound + outbound as a governed broker (net-new)
OMNI points outward as a **controlled broker, default-deny, low-agency, human-gated for consequential acts — never an open mesh:**
- **Inbound** (external / patient / partner agent → OMNI): registration + risk quantification + **signed scoped capability contract** + supply-chain vetting of MCP servers + inbound content treated as hostile-by-default (indirect injection). 
- **Outbound** (OMNI agent → partner tools/agents): MCP/API for tools, A2A for partner agents — under capability envelope + JIT credentials, CIBA/human gate for high-impact.
- **Cross-federation handshake:** signed capability contracts (not OAuth-SSO reuse, not agent-cards-alone — insecure interagent comms is OWASP-agent #7); existing permeability admission **composes** with agent identity + delegation + capability contract. Internet-scale agent identity is industry-unsolved → OMNI governs within its boundary + bilateral contracts.

### §A.5 Cross-operator coordination (sharpened from §7.8)
Default = passive coherence (OMNI Patient CNS observe + signal); active coordination requires an explicit `care_coordination_owner` (preserved). v3 addition: an **agent may be the mechanism** of passive-coherence signaling or *assist* an active coordinator, but `care_coordination_owner` stays a **human/operator role** — agents propose/route; humans/operators own (same discipline as §7.2).

### §A.6 OMNI Direct: architectural rail, economic engine (the distinction)
**Architecturally,** OMNI Direct is one first-party rail among many; the architectural center is the trust axis (Boundary + Context + Authority + Commit). **Economically, OMNI Direct and the owned care surfaces are NOT demoted** — they are a, possibly the, primary demand + trust + revenue origination engine, for years, possibly the premium tier permanently. Conflating the two is the error (`D0THES-GRD-032`); the owned-care economic engine funds and seeds the outward substrate (de-risking the platform-with-no-demand trap).

## §B The AI substrate axis (v3 NEW, first-class; absorbs + expands v2 §9/§12.8/§9.1)

### §B.1 AI's three placements + the bounded-participant doctrine (preserved + framed)
- **Within** the app (runtime): extraction, messaging assist, clinical/medical analysis, candidate generation, context assembly, patient/provider agents.
- **On** the app (build-time): the agentic Build OS — agents building/maintaining OMNI under proof/rules.
- **Ongoing** manual work: us + agents doing architecture/doctrine.
AI remains a **bounded participant**: AI proposes / drafts / extracts / classifies / routes; **humans + owning domains commit** (inherited §9/§12.8 substrate-vs-care boundary, locked). Model-pluggable at substrate, never at the care level. **Code owns control flow + safety; the LLM owns interpretation/drafting** (generative-computing discipline).

### §B.2 Context is a routed strategy (the load-bearing AI doctrine)
*Better context is not more context; the hardest part isn't generation — it's deciding what to look at.* Context is a **routed strategy**, not one mechanism: per workflow, OMNI selects a `context_mode` (scoped packet / retrieval / long-context / hybrid / cached-stable / deterministic / **forbidden**) by safety, cost, accuracy, source-grounding, and auditability. The CNS owns a **Retrieval Decision Layer / Context Router**; retrieval is policy-gated (Federation/RBAC/consent/operator-boundary shape *where* an agent may look); cached context is a performance artifact, **not truth**. This threads the **Sense loop** (inherited §8): context assembly is the governed step before candidate generation.

### §B.3 The Capability / Model / Tool plane (promotes §12.8; AI #12 from deferred)
A governed plane OMNI **exposes and consumes**: `capability_server` / `tool` / `resource` / `prompt` / `schema` / `visibility_policy` / `version` / `call` / `result` / `trace`; `ai_model_registry` + `capability_envelope` + `model_policy_version` (inherited); a **model/optimization router** (reasoning-budget by risk tier; model-as-a-service gateway — no raw provider calls in production); prompt-template registry; **compiled skills** (author in language, deploy as governed software). Runtime discovery is **policy-filtered, scoped, versioned** — never raw `tools/list`. MCP/A2A/API are **rails (transport), not authority** — the care-governance semantics live above them.

### §B.4 CNS = governed agentic orchestration / control plane (sharpens §7.6; bounded)
CNS is OMNI's **governed agentic orchestration / control plane** for candidates, context, tools, traces, and escalation — scheduler, memory tiers (working/semantic/procedural/episodic), tool/identity managers, observability, guardrails, kill switches. **It is NOT a sovereign brain** (`D0THES-GRD-029`): owning domains commit truth, Federation/RBAC gate authority, surfaces/projections own no truth; candidate→resolver→owning-domain-commit is binding. Multi-agent work = bounded roles (planner/doer/tool-op/learner/critic/supervisor/presenter) under CNS, via substrate-mediated handoffs — not free agent chatter; A2A only via CNS-governed orchestration. Deterministic anchors (PII filter, kill switch, clinical/financial gates) stay coded.

### §B.5 Autonomy, HITL, evals, lineage (extends §9.1)
- **Autonomy levels (A0–X)** declared per touchpoint; **HITL / HOTL / HOOTL** as runtime authority modes with autonomy graduation earned by evals/traces; **least agency** (principle): minimum tools/context/authority per role.
- **Evals = runtime proof:** component + E2E + adversarial/red-team + retrieval-quality + replay + regression; **LLM-as-judge = evidence, never authority.** No agentic workflow ships without an eval bundle. Agents are *probabilistic software* — eval distributions, not single-run unit tests.
- **Model lineage extended (§9.1):** every AI-influenced `clinical_adoption` carries `model_version_of_record`; the eval/trace lineage makes the full chain (capability/model/version/prompt/policy/sources/confidence/human-validator/action) reconstructable.
- **Failure modes governed** (OWASP-LLM + OWASP-agent): prompt injection (direct/indirect), excessive agency, goal hijack, tool misuse, memory poisoning, supply-chain, insecure interagent comms, cascading failures, rogue agents, unbounded consumption/denial-of-wallet — countered by zero-trust AI perimeter, AI gateway/DLP, sandboxed tools, secrets-not-in-prompts, cost/loop caps, provenance/chain-of-custody.

### §B.6 The moat discipline (the guard)
Everything generic here (agent OS, MCP, model routing, vector/RAG, IAM, zero-trust) is **commodity by 2030** — OMNI **consumes** it behind the governed boundary. The moat is the **care-governance semantics** layered on top. A sentence about OMNI's AI or trust layer that would read identically for a bank or logistics company is commodity, not moat.

## §C Outward interoperability posture (v3 NEW)

OMNI exposes and consumes a **designed, bounded set** of governed capabilities (context-packet, eligibility, intake, booking, message-draft, document, observation-read, fulfillment, consent/grant) — expanded by deliberate doctrine pass, never "any agent can ask anything" (extends `D0THES-GRD-003`). **Architect the boundary as a first-class plane now** (cheap now, catastrophic to retrofit — the crypto-agility lesson); **expose thin for the wedge, grow with demand** (full agent/federation mesh = 2028-2032+). Owned orchestration/control plane comes **before** outward exposure (else the fortress reappears as point-to-point spaghetti). **"Governed handshake layer" is a descriptive facet, never the identity** — OMNI is context + authority + ownership + proof + commit, not thin brokering (`D0THES-GRD-032`).

**$1B / 2035-2040 arc:** 2026-28 owned wedge + substrate (boundary architected, thin exposure) → 2028-32 partner operators + first external/patient agents transacting through the governed boundary (the moat compounds) → 2032-40 OMNI is the governed care-context-and-action substrate others integrate, with first-party care still running as proof + premium.
