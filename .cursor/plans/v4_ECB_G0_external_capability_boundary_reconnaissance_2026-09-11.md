## §2 — The question

> **What operating model must OMNI realize so that people and independently governed systems can turn changing, incomplete context into timely, safe, accountable care and supporting business action — across individual, shared-operational, and population/network settings, including generated therapies and adaptive physical systems — and which existing, revised, or new mechanisms make that possible?**

**A valid result may conclude that the inherited candidate is sufficient, needs named extensions, needs a different decomposition, or is more elaborate than a requirement warrants. All four must remain reachable.** The arc is not a recovery exercise, a `REV-188` ticket closure, a universal industry-ontology project, or a rewrite of native domain lifecycles.

### §2.1 Two statements that must both survive, and must not be merged

The operator's instruction: *"if we derived the actual ambition, celebrated it, then diluted it down with the guardrail framing and discarded the ambition... then future us drifts, future us doesn't see the ambition."* **Correct, and the fix is not to choose between them.** Both are recorded, separately labelled, with an explicit rule that the second bounds *how* we pursue the first and **does not replace it**.

**THE AMBITION** — a declared strategic intention (`strategic hypothesis` basis, `§4`):

> **OMNI exists to make care act at the speed of the decision and the safety of the best available evidence — simultaneously — from a moisturizer sale to a trauma suite. Not a faster chart. Not a safer chart. An operating substrate on which a commitment can be made the moment it is warranted, and reconsidered the moment it should be, without anyone re-assembling the world by conversation first.** If OMNI cannot eventually stand behind *"we decomposed the physics, built the relationships, and built it for speed and safety"*, it has not earned its position.

**THE CONTRACT** — the bounded mechanism by which the ambition is pursued, and the only thing this arc may test:

> **OMNI is designed to reduce avoidable delay and preventable error by maintaining trustworthy, source-attributed context and explicit conditions for action; reusing what remains valid; rechecking what may invalidate the next act; coordinating resources and independent commitments; and carrying material changes to the authorities responsible for responding. It must remain honest about what is unknown and preserve necessary judgment, consent, verification, and continuing responsibility.**

**The relationship, stated so neither erodes the other:** the ambition names what we are reaching for and may be quoted by the company. The contract names what we will actually build and be measured on. **A guardrail that deletes the reach is a governance failure; a reach that hides the guardrail is a safety failure.** Both go to their homes in `§13.4`.

**And the target is timely *appropriate* action, not transaction velocity.** A faster unnecessary intervention is not an improvement. Sometimes the correct output is immediate action; sometimes fresh evidence, deliberation, refusal, or preparing adjacent work while one condition stays open.

## §2.2 — Who is this for? The three-way split that Amazon does not have

The operator's observation: OMNI has always been conceived as *"the providers buy OMNI and record what they do — even though the recipient is the patient,"* and in the Amazon analogy *"the customer plays on the provider's field, not the other way around."* **The structural reason healthcare never resolves "who is the app for" is that three roles Amazon fuses are split here.**

| Role | Amazon | Care |
|---|---|---|
| **Beneficiary** — who the act is for | the customer | **the patient**, who is also a rights-holder, not an asset whose owner sets the objective |
| **Purchaser** — who buys and deploys the system | the customer | **the provider or institution** |
| **Authority** — who may decide the act | the customer | **the licensed clinician**, bounded by institutional policy, patient consent and payer coverage |

**Amazon fuses all three in one party, which is why its architecture can be customer-rooted. Care splits them three ways, which is why OMNI cannot be "for" any single one of them without breaking.** A provider-only OMNI loses the patient's rights-holding. A patient-only OMNI loses professional authority and the institution's duty. **The architecture must serve the *relationship*, not a party** — which is the correct generalization of the original 1:1 promise (*one provider exerting judgment for one patient using one app*), not a contradiction of it.

## §2.3 — Brokerage versus duty: the answer to the OpenAI scenario

The operator's scenario: in 2040 the patient enters the OR confident because forty-three vendors aligned, each proved its worth, a third-party oversight app cross-referenced them, and **they all reported back to the patient's hub on someone else's platform, not to OMNI.** *"That is actually 2040 healthcare, no?"* And his own counter, which is sharper than he credits: *"we wouldn't go to a Panda Express and expect a Michelin meal because they've never organized that. We have organized it 1000 times — meaning Henry Ford has."*

**What "duty" means here — the operator's formulation, which supersedes both a legal test and an obligation taxonomy by sitting at a different altitude (`§16`):**

> **A duty is the deliverable promised to the patient, at a stated scope and horizon.** Henry Ford, while the patient is in brain surgery, has undertaken *"get the tumour out of this patient's intracranium."* An OMNI-powered peptide clinic has undertaken *"get the testosterone to this patient's door."* It may also be *"keep me healthy"* or *"help me lose forty pounds."* **Everything that occurs under the hood to make that deliverable happen is what this arc studies.**

**This is an entry into the work, NOT a master object.** A cardinality law and a lifecycle law were previously derived from it and are withdrawn (`§16`); Care capture explicitly rejects *"Care never closes,"* and an ongoing relationship carries review, renewal, transfer, withdrawal and closure conditions while the person's broader goal continues. **Converting an operator's framing into an ontology is this arc's recurring failure.**

**What the framing does earn is a set of four things that must stay distinguishable** — and the arc's subject is the machinery between them:

| | |
|---|---|
| **what the patient wants** | *"help me lose forty pounds"* — a desired result, which by itself establishes nothing about who promised what |
| **what a participant actually undertook** | the scoped promise — the clinic's, the clinician's, the pharmacy's, the software operator's; several parties may undertake different parts |
| **what work and dependencies support it** | the entire subject of this arc |
| **what was delivered, and what was observed** | **shipping the product is a distinguishable deliverable and does not close the clinical or follow-up work**; the tumour example is a description of intended care, **never an unconditional guarantee of a biological outcome** |

**`Q-DUTY`:** which of these four must OMNI represent explicitly, at what scope and horizon, and where does the estate already carry them? **A revealing test for any successor: can this framing be used without forcing every shared resource, research opportunity, supplier relationship or population-level change beneath one patient undertaking?** If not, the framing is being over-applied again.

**Inherited position on "who is this for."** The CNS contract's governing phrase answers the operator's provider-versus-patient question more precisely than anything this thread produced: *"**Workstreams have operators. Patients have coherence. Networks have governance.**"* (`§7.6`, `draft_for_ratification`). Its cross-operator section then separates **passive coherence** from **explicitly accepted active coordination** — by a named operator, a navigation service, a partner, **or the patient themselves** within scope. **So the inherited picture is neither "a patient logs in and OMNI commands everyone who treats them" nor "providers buy a chart and patients are passive."** A defensible working formulation: **OMNI supplies the operating substrate through which accountable participants perform and coordinate care and supporting operations, while preserving the patient's context, participation, rights and continuity across those arrangements.** Primary buyer, primary user, beneficiary, initiating actor and authority remain **different questions** (`§2.2`). → `Q-FOR`.

**The comparative question, stated without a categorical answer.** Offering an option is not the same act as accepting responsibility for its performance, and that distinction is worth keeping. **It does not settle the competitive question.** A previous formulation tried to, by label test; withdrawn (`§16`).

**No label proves adequacy or inadequacy.** A competing arrangement can combine a patient-facing hub, operating software, contracted delivery organizations and accountable clinicians. The comparison must therefore be run against **the whole arrangement** — what it supplies, which responsibilities remain with which participants, what it costs, and what it does when a participant refuses, disappears or withholds internals — not against whether the component called "broker" is secretly a provider. **An intermediary can also carry obligations of its own without being the treating clinician**, so "infrastructure" is not a duty-free category either.

**What stays open, and is `Q-ROLE` / `H-ROLE`:** what OMNI supplies in a given arrangement, what participants retain, where OMNI participates directly versus depends on another party versus stays outside — and whether accumulated accountable practice (credentialing, review, accreditation, institutional memory) is something OMNI can make legible and portable across participants, or something that stays with the party that accepted the undertaking. **Hub ownership and duty acceptance are two different questions and neither answers the other.**

## §3 — The speed mechanism: what commitment solves, and what it does not

**Three earlier claims are withdrawn (`§16`)** — that preconditions become *"continuously known,"* that a commitment then needs no re-verification, and that a change reaches *"exactly"* the dependent decisions. What survives is narrower and testable.

### §3.1 What the tray example proves

Two procedures both read *"one sterile tray available."* **Both satisfy a precondition check. Neither can safely proceed.** A more frequently refreshed indicator does not help — this is time-of-check/time-of-use, and the fix is not a fresher check. **The fix is that one of them must be able to HOLD the tray.**

What the tray case establishes is narrow and solid: **for contention over a scarce excludable resource, a freshness improvement does not make two actors safe, and some form of authoritative hold does.** *"One tray available"* is an observation; *"this tray is held for this case until T"* is a commitment, and only the second supports a promise.

**It does not establish that reservation explains speed generally** — previously asserted, withdrawn (`§16`). Reservation is **one candidate mechanism** among several the investigation must distinguish: evidence reuse · earlier acquisition of a missing fact · reduced reconstruction · preparation and parallelism · safe retry · selective reconsideration · fewer unnecessary handoffs. **Accepting responsibility to perform work is also not the same semantic object as an exclusive resource reservation**, and a payment idempotency key is a third thing again. The industrial comparators are **suggestive design analogies, not established explanations of healthcare delay** — they enter under `§4`'s basis discipline as *externally informed*, and `§12.2` measures which mechanism actually applies per operation rather than assuming one.

### §3.2 The corrected mechanism

**ECB's proposed analytic grouping of `EVRUN-000012 §15`'s fourteen Layer-4 relations — basis `inferred`, NOT source-derived** (`§13.0` intake; **`_02`'s own `§15.4`** — an external section reference, not this carrier's — lists them as a flat set with no grouping). It separates kinds this arc had conflated:

| Kind | Relations | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| **Evaluation** — is this condition supported by available evidence? | `requires` · `depends_on` · `permits` · `constrains` | knowing whether to proceed; **never a guarantee about the world** |
| **Commitment** — has something been undertaken or held for this act? | `offered_to` · `accepted_by` · `actualizes` · `blocks` | the ability to **promise**. **A relation's name does not establish reservation semantics** — accepting responsibility to perform work and holding a resource exclusively are different objects (`§3.1`), and which of these relations carries which is a `D3` question, not a given |
| **Consequence** — what changes when something a decision rested on changes? | `invalidates` · `signals_reconsideration` · `supersedes` · `compensates` · `remediates` | the ability to **reconsider correctly**; the basis of safety |
| **Disclosure** | `exposes` | **the fourteenth relation, omitted from the R6–R15 grouping.** It is the one most relevant to Layer-5 projections and to outward disclosure at `§6.3`/`H-CIRC`, and its omission removed it from both analyses |

> **For contention over a scarce, excludable resource:** speed comes from holding a commitment rather than re-reading availability · safety comes from knowing what to reconsider when a held commitment breaks or a relied-upon fact changes · efficiency comes from discovering infeasibility at planning time rather than at execution. **This is bounded — `§3.2a` states what it does NOT explain, and delay reduction is a portfolio.**

**One candidate explanation, to be tested and not assumed:** that across organizational boundaries a material class of coordination delay persists because no party can *hold* the tray, the OR minute, the supplier allocation or the pre-auth, so each step is renegotiated at the moment of need. **It competes with the other mechanisms at `§3.1` and is not the baseline account of healthcare delay.** `H-SPEED`, discriminated at `§12.2`.

### §3.2a — What commitment does NOT solve

**Held commitments were previously presented as the general explanation of speed; withdrawn as a universal claim (`§16`).** The mechanism applies to **contention over scarce, excludable, reservable things** — an instrument, an OR minute, a manufacturing slot, a supplier allocation, a staffing block. **It does not extend to:**

| Not solvable by commitment | Because | What it needs instead |
|---|---|---|
| a physiological variable | **you cannot reserve a blood pressure.** Several interventions may legitimately affect one variable *simultaneously* | a validated joint configuration, constraint, supervision or sequencing model — `§6` |
| a future biological response | **a manufacturing slot can be reserved; a patient's response cannot** | scientific evidence and models, with uncertainty preserved |
| an interpretation | meaning changes without the material changing | source authority, adoption state, supersession |
| a fact that is stable but costly to obtain | a stable fact can still dominate the critical path | better observation, reuse, earlier preparation, parallel work |

**And a reservation can itself expire, be revoked, or become unavailable** — holding is not permanence. **A payment idempotency key and an inventory reservation are not the same semantic object**, and were previously used interchangeably (`§16`). **So delay reduction is a portfolio: commitments for contention, better observation and reuse for reconstruction, preparation and parallelism for lead time, appropriate automation for routine transfer, and change detection for attention.** Which combination applies is per-operation, and `§12.2` measures it rather than assuming it.

**And "continuously evaluable" is not "continuously known."** A system maintains an assessment of declared conditions against available information; it cannot know reality across independent organizations, humans, devices and scientific processes. `REV-184` already holds the world model to be a **partial, time-stamped projection** with autonomy bounded by a trust horizon and out-of-band action preserved. **An observation being older than now does not make it false or unusable — freshness is action-relative.** A collection time stays a valid historical fact; an availability assertion stops being usable the moment someone else reserves the resource; an interpretation can change while the material does not. **No universal evidence half-life.** `§6` lists the temporal dimensions to test per profile rather than as required columns.

## §4 — Exposing the basis of a claim

**These four distinctions were previously a mandatory exclusive box; withdrawn as an enum (`§16`).** A requirement may legitimately combine physical limitation, institutional obligation, local policy, ethical commitment and empirical assumption — **mixed basis is not a defect.** Internal policy can be changed by its authorized owner; a model of reality can simply be wrong.

**The discipline is to expose the basis, not to file the claim.** When a claim is load-bearing, say which of these it rests on and in what proportion: **reality** (incomplete observation, irreversible action, consumed material, elapsed time) · **institutional/legal** (professional authority, licensure, data-use obligation, research/practice distinction) · **chosen commitment** (portability, neutrality, non-privilege, refusal to broker) · **empirical/strategic** (defensibility, adoption, burden reduction, measured benefit).

**The failure this prevents:** carrying a commercial hope as though it were a law of nature, which makes it unfalsifiable. **The failure it must not create:** ceremony that forces a box onto every invariant.

## §5 — Reality, and OMNI's relation to it

The operator asked whether we have ever declared this. **Not as one statement; the pieces exist in `REV-184` and C4.5.** Proposed integration — **not a new domain, plane or service:**

> **Reality includes people, physical processes, independent institutions, and events that may occur outside OMNI's knowledge or control.** OMNI maintains source-attributed observations, claims, adopted meanings, commitments, occurrences and derived views; **each view has a scope, a time basis, an authority basis, and stated limits.** Authorized humans and systems act through **bounded control relationships**; resulting evidence updates those views and may create, fulfil, revise or transfer obligations. **OMNI must distinguish the world, its representation of the world, its permitted influence on the world, and its evidence of what actually happened.**

The operator's *"stale microseconds later"* intuition needs one refinement: **the question is never whether a representation is current, but whether it still supports this particular use.** Perfect knowledge is not a prerequisite for bounded action, and uncertainty is not permission to act without an adequate basis.

### §5.1 The snapshot question — one word covering three kinds of artifact and four distinct jobs

The operator: *"what are these reality packets or whatever that OMNI is or isn't delivering... here you go robot... here is the pre-op snapshot... is that a thing? do other companies do that?"* **The two tables below cut the same territory differently and are not meant to agree in count** — the first distinguishes **kinds of artifact** by direction and status, the second distinguishes **jobs** that may be composed across one or more artifacts. **Whether a gap exists here is unresolved**, not established: it becomes a composition question once the sources in `§13.2` are reconciled (`Q-BRIEF-2`). Three kinds of artifact are being called "snapshot":

| **`as_of` reconstruction** | backward | what we can defensibly say we knew as of T, computed over versioned sources | C4.5 charter accepted; **pass not started** |

**"A copy of state is exactly the wrong shape" is withdrawn (`§16`)** — it contradicted this carrier's own rule that older information is not automatically unusable, and substituted a permission for information. They do different jobs and must not replace one another:

| Role | Job | Cannot do |
|---|---|---|

**A clearance does not contain the patient's anatomy. An image does not authorize an incision. A reservation does not establish a physiological condition.** These may be composed or referenced together without becoming four new universal objects.

**One requirement, from the operator's video-plus-telemetry example: a single packet timestamp must not imply that everything inside it was observed simultaneously.** The consumer needs the per-observation times, the clock or sequence basis that supports correspondence, and the residual uncertainty. **And neither realization dominates:** a reference that becomes unretrievable is not superior to a copy that remains usable; a copy that is stale at execution is not superior to a live read. **Fitness for the act decides, per profile.**

**Absence claim corrected.** *"The forward-looking object does not exist"* was too strong (`§16`). CNS `§9.1` already carries operational authority-labelled context packets with a context snapshot for audit-replay, and the estate carries a **`capability_envelope`** family — *"capability contract · authority ceiling · typed side effects · evals · versioning · proof"* — plus **`delegated_authority_envelope`** (*who / scope / TTL / revocation*) under the rule that *"an agent's **claim** to represent a principal is itself a candidate requiring verification … self-asserted agent authority is never trusted."* `v4_C4_agent_runtime_and_harness_capture.md` states the AI-substrate work **already named** the *Capability Envelope Contract* and *Context Packet* deliverables in **thesis v3 §B** — **which this author has never read.** The surviving claim is narrow: **no bounded permission-to-act object has been reconciled against that family, and whether one is needed is a G1 question** (`Q-BRIEF-2`). The useful shape to test, whatever its home: the minimum sufficient facts and their time basis (`T-15`) · **what must remain true throughout execution, not merely at authorization** · the authority under which the act proceeds and its scope · the **held commitments** it rests on (`§3.2`) · the fallback if a condition fails mid-act · an expiry, and who may revoke it early.

**Do other industries have this? Yes, and it is not called a snapshot.** A **takeoff clearance** is actor-specific, time-bounded, condition-bearing, revocable, and contains none of the airspace. A **permit-to-work** names the task, the isolations that must hold for its duration, the authorizing person, the expiry and the conditions that void it. **The mechanism is a clearance, not a data extract.** Whether OMNI already has an equivalent is **unresolved** — see the absence correction below. `Q-BRIEF`.

**The estate's nearest relative is CNS `§9.1`'s layered context packet, which already *references rather than copies* and preserves authority — the right instinct, but assembled for orchestration, not issued as a bounded permission to act.** Whether an action brief is a profile of that object or a distinct one is `Q-BRIEF-2`, and whether it is the same object as GCE's boundary exchange is `Q-BOUNDARY-2`.

## §6 — The six-robot trauma case: where does OMNI sit?

The operator's escalation is the right one and it must be answered concretely: a trauma suite with a vascular robot, an ortho hip robot, a lab-draw robot, a ventilator, a VTE prophylaxis device and continuous imaging — **three vendors and operating systems, five humans with their own AI agents, a device rep, backend vendors, the lab, the pre-op note, PACU status.** Six actuators on one body at once.

### §6.1 What actually couples them — and it is not their software

**The patient's body, the shared room, the shared resources, and the clock.** The vascular repair changes hemodynamics the ventilator is compensating. The ortho robot's positioning changes the vascular robot's working geometry. Imaging demands a room-wide hold and adds exposure to everyone present. VTE prophylaxis interacts with the bleeding being controlled. The lab result is stale before it is read. **None of that coupling runs through an integration.** Connecting their software makes the coupling visible and manageable — and also lets mistakes propagate faster.

**So the problem is not message routing — and it is not settled that it is authority allocation either.** *(previously organised as arbitration of rights over a shared physical variable; withdrawn as the general model — `§16`)* **You can hold an instrument; you cannot hold a blood pressure in the same sense**, several interventions may legitimately act on one variable simultaneously, and a right to command one actuator is not ownership of every variable that actuator influences. Authority allocation, **coordinated simultaneous action, sequencing, inhibition, supervision, a shared constraint, and a different validated configuration** are all candidate mechanisms; which applies is a clinical and control-engineering question and **is not derivable from a custody vocabulary.**

**The questions that actually have to be answered:** which **combinations** of behaviour are permitted · which assumptions each participant relies on · which changes require coordination · which conditions must hold **throughout** execution · who can detect their failure · and what the arrangement may safely do then. → `H-ARB`, `Q-ARB-2`.

**One observation, carrying no mechanism selection.** Aviation uses positive transfer of control; an OR runs on the surgeon's authority and the anesthesiologist's veto, executed verbally. **Whether that is *the* mechanism, one of several, or a human practice that a governed substrate should not try to represent at all is open** — and whether OMNI already carries any of it is unestablished rather than absent (`§13.0` ①). `Q-ARB`.

### §6.2 Three answers, one of which is a real competitive threat

**Three conclusions previously asserted here are withdrawn and become alternatives to evaluate (`§16`).**

**(a) OMNI's placement is OPEN, not "never in a control loop."** The sound constraint is narrow: **no unexamined remote round trip inside immediate device safety**, and a device's safe local completion must not silently close broader clinical or business obligations. **It was previously generalized into a permanent prohibition, erasing the openness C3.5 F5 deliberately preserved** — *"a per-system, per-context governed decision — not a thesis assumption."* Live alternatives, each with different authority, timing, assurance and regulatory consequences and **none authorized here:** an external qualified bedside coordinator with OMNI supplying context, episode coordination and longitudinal consequence handling · **a qualified OMNI-supplied local component** under declared clinical and technical authority with its own safety, timing, fault and regulatory assessment · a validated distributed arrangement with explicit interaction guarantees · more centralized integration operated by a hospital, consortium, manufacturer or independent integrator.

**(b) The god robot — a real competitive path.** Its previous refutation — *"works only while one vendor owns every actuator"* and *"no vendor can be trusted to arbitrate a competitor"* — **is withdrawn as assertion against counterevidence (`§16`).** Reportedly MD PnP demonstrated remote control of **three brands of infusion pump through one OpenICE interface** (2023 research demonstration), and Dräger announced a **multi-vendor** ventilation/monitoring/infusion/alarm arrangement (2025, vendor-reported, geographically bounded). **Neither proves an autonomous trauma suite; both rebut categorical impossibility.** *(Relayed, unverified here — `§13.2`.)* **A manufacturer, hospital, consortium or independent integrator can operate a coordinating system under accepted responsibilities. Commercial conflict is a reason for governance and evidence, not proof of technical impossibility.**

**(c) Holding the record is not holding the authority.** *"Whoever holds the arbitration record holds the seat"* is withdrawn (`§16`). **A recorder can faithfully capture a decision made elsewhere; its database does not become the decision-maker.** Reliance has to be earned operationally — **strategic control cannot be inferred from being the place where events are logged.** What survives is narrower and still valuable: the arrangement needs a **record of what each participant was told, when, and whether it acted on information already superseded** — and someone has to hold it.

**(d) "We will just record it" is not an adequate fallback**, and the previous position that OMNI records rather than arbitrates where a participant refuses is insufficient (`§16`). **Reduced visibility and a missing safety condition are different failures, and naming a state "degraded" does not make it safe.** If an essential participant refuses a safety-required coordination protocol, a qualified authority must determine whether the arrangement proceeds in **another accepted mode**, requires a **human-directed mode**, a **different participant**, **delay**, **transfer**, or a **bounded emergency response** — or **must not begin.** An action already underway raises different questions from one not yet started. → `Q-ARB-4`, `Q-REFUSE`.

## §6.3 — Intelligence that returns through a person: a normal design case

The operator's case: the patient or family screenshots everything OMNI shows them into their own model and asks *"did the robot, the lab, the OMNI intelligence and the surgeon do this right?"*, then brings the answer back verbally. **This is not an exception to a closed system. It is the standard cycle and it must be designed for.**

`OMNI presents → someone consults an independently chosen service → that service interprets → the person returns a concern through speech, a message, a screenshot or an upload → the care arrangement reviews and dispositions it → any resulting change is separately committed and followed through.`

**Influence crosses the boundary with no machine-to-machine integration at all.** What OMNI must be able to preserve: *the patient reports that an outside service questioned this result · the submitted image appears to concern this source and date · we do or do not hold the underlying reasoning and inputs · the concern has been routed to this kind of review · here is its disposition and any resulting care change.*

**Two rules, both directions.** **Do not require disclosure of a private AI conversation before a concern can be heard** — unknown provenance is a limitation to represent, not grounds to dismiss the substance. **And the outside answer acquires no command authority by being pasted into an OMNI message.** A concern can deserve urgent attention without its originator being entitled to dictate the response. **The adjacent estate position, stated at its actual scope rather than as a universal prohibition.** Returned AI content is **not self-authorizing**, and any resulting action requires the appropriate authority and the owning-domain commit path. **That is the boundary.** It is **not** established that every non-human commit is prohibited: the joint-work product model treats **candidate-only behaviour as the default where authority has not been granted** and contemplates bounded execution within a pre-authorized envelope while prohibiting independent alteration of clinical meaning — with the envelope grammar itself an **unresolved downstream obligation**. **Carry that qualification into RECONCILE rather than opening the arc with either a universal prohibition or a universal permission**, keeping capability, delegated execution, clinical adoption and authoritative commitment distinct. *(This does not reopen FAI's paused authority question, which stays with FAI — see the pinned pause context at `§12.9.1`.)*

**The privacy boundary changes shape.** An independently chosen consumer application is not governed by the same arrangement as a provider-deployed assistant, and information a patient voluntarily enters elsewhere may sit outside the regulated relationship entirely — *(relayed, unverified, `§13.2`; this carrier makes no legal determination)*. **Architecturally: OMNI cannot promise its access controls follow a screenshot.** The honest product direction is **better provenance on the way out** — preserve dates, units, source status, missing context and correction references in exports — and to **distinguish revoking future access from retracting an already-disclosed copy**, and to **resolve a family member's representative role rather than assuming "family" supplies permission.**

**The deeper hazard is circular corroboration.** A robot reports *task completed*; a summary calls the procedure *successful*; the family's model reads that summary; a clinician's assistant later reads the family model's output and treats it as corroboration. **Four statements, one observation, and one unjustified semantic upgrade.** Care `§9a` already carries the rule — *multiplicity ≠ independence*, with shared sources, shared model families, copied contributions and unknown correlation — **and it has never been tested through a full round trip.** **The mirror case matters equally: apparent disagreement often comes from different dates, missing inputs, units, versions or questions.** Establish whether two outputs answer the same question from comparable evidence **before** trying to reconcile them. **OMNI should preserve meaningful disagreement, detect false corroboration, and route review — never manufacture consensus among models.** → `H-CIRC`, `Q-RETURN`.

## §9 — Frontier pressures *(not reducible to one axis)*

R5 reduced the frontier to shortened precondition lifetimes. **Too narrow — a stable fact can be expensive to obtain, reconcile or apply, and a fast-changing fact can be irrelevant to the next act.** The pressures to preserve together: **generated interventions** (the capability may not exist until context, specimens, experimentation and design produce it) · **adaptive execution** (conditions change during an action; admission and initial authorization settle nothing ongoing) · **coupled participants** (independently acceptable actions interacting through physiology, resources, deadlines or objectives) · **multiple scales** (a local observation, batch, model release or policy change affecting many people and organizations) · **independent authority and incomplete visibility** (participants refuse terms, withhold intermediates, change posture, disappear) · **long-lived consequences** (exposure, monitoring, correction and responsibility surviving the original episode and its participants) · **and shortened validity where it genuinely occurs.** These are proof obligations, not predictions that every capability arrives by a date.

## §11 — Scenario generation

**Not a library.** **Opus proposes cases; an independent reviewer challenges the coverage model, supplies held-out cases and rejects incorrect expected behaviour; Nick adjudicates.** **Declared coverage is defined once, at `§12.4`, and this section does not restate or compete with it.** The axes below are **selection dimensions**: they guide the choice of discriminating extremes and interactions *within* an obligation — **they do not multiply the obligations.** Coverage is never row count, justified inapplicability stands, and one case may discharge several obligations — **and the coverage model itself must be attacked**, because a finite factor model can omit the relevant actor, sequence, physical effect or institutional behaviour. Not every case deserves equal depth or its own artifact.

**§11.1 Axes** — `track_mix` · `owner_spread` · `regime` · `production_locus` · `patient_specificity` · `capability_existence` · `execution_agency` · **`concurrency_on_a_shared_variable`** · **`commitment_availability`** · `adaptivity` · `control_timescale_spread` · `irreversibility` · `obligation_horizon` · `principal_count` · `regulatory_settledness` · `producer_permanence` · `institutional_willingness` · `disclosure_openness` · `initiating_principal` · `participant_ownership` · `connectivity`.

**§11.2 Breaker families** — **commitment and contention** (two actors one resource; check-then-use; expiry; revocation; stale hold) · **joint activity** (two or more participants acting on one patient or variable at once; interacting effects; timing; conflicting objectives; comms loss; acting on superseded information — **the coordination mechanism is an output, not a premise**) · **duration** (a condition that must hold throughout execution fails mid-act) · track and ownership misrouting · readiness and stale readiness · time and validity (sample ≠ analysis ≠ receipt; stale authorization; changed scene; changed-during-manufacture) · duplication and routing (one event two paths treated as corroboration; repeated delivery causing repeated action; route mistaken for authority) · authority and impersonation (statement→command; extraction→authority; ACK→custody; acceptance→execution; execution→effect; release→adoption; model evidence→observed response) · participation change (join, leave, revoke, unknown-at-start, refusal, withdrawal with work in flight) · affiliation and topology · degradation · continuity and disappearance · **population and propagation, including unrecorded reliance** · capability formation · neutrality and incentive · **honest-null** (no intervention, no eligibility, refusal — a valid outcome, not a failure).

**§11.3 Regimes — all three mandatory** — **individual** arrangement with changing participants · **shared operational** (lots, equipment, facilities, capacity, protocols, model releases, supplier arrangements, **operations with zero identified patients**) · **cross-organization and population** (learning, collective hazards, propagation, privacy at scale). **Running the first repeatedly cannot prove the other two.**

**§11.4 Positive capability tests — mandatory** — a patient discovers a legitimate opportunity · a supplier receives enough to fulfil a real need · **an operator reserves resources and the commitment holds** · **a commitment is made immediately because it is held, not because the world is known** · care continues under an acceptable alternative · coordination happens without unnecessary gates. **A framework that only prevents action is not an operating model.**

**§11.5 Negative controls — predicates, not labels.** **The illegal substitution is the failure; the first state in each pair is not wrong.** An ACK as an ACK is valid — as accepted custody it is not. A simulation labelled as such is valid — presented as observed response it is not. A refusal recorded as refusal is valid — as an intervention performed it is not. Likewise: **an availability observation vs a held reservation** · approved candidate vs actual exposure · manufacturer's release vs clinical adoption · stale authorization recognised vs relied upon · one event deduplicated vs double-counted or double-executed · affected party reached vs missed, unaffected notified as exposed · evidence in purpose vs secondary use · an attributed clinical statement vs a device command · an OMNI-operated participant on identical terms vs privileged · a real operating difference represented vs flattened. **A written trace is design evidence — not proof of a deployed system, counterparty agreement, or clinical safety.**

**§11.6 Required scenario families, and the home for evaluation specifications and results.** *(Created empty here; populated at TRACE preparation per `§12.9.6`.)*

> **Execution obligation carried from the fresh-Knox review — the no-patient regime is NOT discharged by a readiness or inventory witness.** The shared-operations family must exercise the **full business-formation span**: choosing an offering · evaluating supplier representations · adopting catalog meaning · setting commercial terms and consent applicability · publishing an effective configuration · **consumption of it** · defect detection · **and downstream correction of what the defect already touched.** The native joint-work adjudication expressly rejected a comparison that stopped configuration at publication while following care through exposure and remedy. **At minimum the operating-change evidence must reach consumption, failure and downstream correction** — and this links `D2`/`D5`. **The individual regime must exercise asymmetric detection at least once** (`H-STAND`, `H-RECORD`): an adverse event detected by some participants and not others, where the detecting set and the duty-bearing set **differ** — including at least one observer with no contract to provider or OMNI. **Test what the arrangement does when the party who saw it owes nothing and the party who owes it did not see it.** **Likewise a large patient count is not by itself a different regime:** population evidence must exercise a changed operating property — partial impact knowledge, competing resource claims, distributed refusal, or responsibility surviving organizational change.

**These are required families, not a case-count target, and they are conditional operating pressures — not predictions about any named vendor's products.**

| Family | What it must discriminate |
|---|---|
| one provider, one patient, external peptide/Rx fulfillment | whether OMNI enables **ordinary work without imposing enterprise machinery**, while preserving independent acceptance, substitution, money and follow-up boundaries |
| personal wearable/glasses interface + independently chosen AI | influence **without an integration** · partial or stale context · private reasoning · patient-returned disagreement · false corroboration · **authority that does not follow the screenshot** |
| patient-specific mRNA, cell-based or analogous generated intervention | a capability **formed from patient inputs** · design and version change · material identity and irreversible work · **release versus adoption** · later monitoring and correction |
| multiple physical systems acting during one procedure | coupled effects · supervision · timing · local versus remote control · missing communication · responsibility after partial execution — **without assuming one owner of the body or one universal coordinator** |
| shared operations with **no identified patient** | resources, capacity, lots, facilities, supplier obligations and operating economics **that cannot be forced beneath a patient episode** |
| population/network learning and long-horizon change | opportunity discovery · purpose-limited use · corrected evidence · affected-case uncertainty · participant exit · continuing obligations across organizations |

**Extension test — required within this coverage.** After an account is formed, **introduce a materially different participant or capability** and show which existing semantics survive, which need a profile or contract change, and whether an architectural change is genuinely required. ***"Everything fits without change" is not the required answer; an explicit governed path for necessary change is.*** **Do not run a futuristic payload through today's workflow and call it a frontier test — the change must be to the arrangement itself.**

**Credible competing whole arrangement — required.** Do not protect OMNI by defining a competitor as records-only, integration-only, or incapable of employing clinicians and contracting with delivery organizations. Keep ECB's operating-model comparison distinct from Task D's wider verdict (`§12.8`).

**The result must be capable of saying: reuse this · simplify that · change this boundary · defer this pending specific evidence · do not undertake this responsibility.**

## §12.5 — The reuse test: early closure must not repeat the failure that opened this arc

R8 said that if Pass 1 finds the inherited account already answers the question, the arc closes at `EXISTING_ARCHITECTURE_SUFFICIENT_WITH_ROUTING`. **That exit is dangerous as written and is now gated.** Pass 1's review stop establishes what inherited evidence exists and what proof debt remains — **inheritance, not operating sufficiency** — and `§12.4` bars it from declaring sufficiency rather than from reading failure-mode evidence. Closing there would license exactly the move this arc exists to prevent: *we found the terminology in earlier work, therefore we need not test the future operating regime.*

**No new architecture is a legitimate result. No new proof because we found a paragraph is not.** So a reuse claim must show, per requirement, that the prior evidence covers **the same requirement · the same assumptions · the same operating conditions · the same relevant failure modes · at the required maturity** (`§5`'s four evidence dimensions). **Prior evidence that was never exercised under multi-participant concurrency, adaptive execution, generated interventions, population propagation or a refusing counterparty does not discharge those requirements** — and those are precisely the pressures the arc was opened for (`§9`).

**Timing.** Pass 1 **stages** this test — it establishes what inherited evidence exists, at what authority and maturity, and what proof debt remains. **It does not close it**, because whether prior evidence covers the relevant failure modes is partly determined by exercising them (Pass 2). A Pass-1 answer of *"insufficient; these tests are owed"* is a complete and expected Pass-1 result. **Closure happens at Pass 3, on the record.**

**And even a full-reuse result still owes Outputs A–D.** The integrated account and the downstream consumption package are the deliverable regardless of whether the answer is "nothing new." **Early closure is a success only when the reuse test passes on the record; it is never a shortcut around the frontier burden.**

### §12.9.4 — What running a test means

**Default: a source-grounded, stepwise design trace.** Any use of an executable model or observed evidence is labelled distinctly. **Prose execution, simulation, implementation and deployed proof are four different things and are never reported as one.**

**The evaluator may not repair an underspecified candidate mid-trace and then score the repaired behaviour as if it had been specified.**

**Expected behaviour needs an attributable basis. Reviewer agreement establishes no biological efficacy, no control-system safety, and no institutional acceptance.** Where those facts are unavailable the test may examine whether the arrangement **preserves the uncertainty and routes the decision correctly** — it cannot award the missing external proof.

**Comparison uses the same demanded outcome, operating span and material conditions.** A whole OMNI arrangement is never compared against a competitor artificially stopped at storage, publication or messaging. **Value evaluation counts work shifted onto patients, clinicians, counterparties and operators**; analytical estimates and synthetic traces stay labelled and produce no measured benefit.

**A method defect discovered mid-evaluation is recorded and reviewed before affected scoring continues; unaffected work does not automatically stop.**
