### §1b — Accountability Loop ≠ incident management
**Runtime Operations owns the *service incident*** (restore/stabilize; can restore without solving the defect and without a party being owed a remedy). **This loop owns the *obligation*** (custody + remedy + communication + verified closure). A `service_incident` (Runtime) / `product_defect` (Platform Change Engineering) **crosses in only at a consequence/duty threshold** (§18); it stays canonical in its owning plane; this loop holds a reference + projections + (if a party is owed) a `response_case`.

## §2 — Coordinator ≠ ledger (process ≠ durable state)

Rides CNS in normal mode; must run **out-of-band when CNS/normal surfaces — or the coordinator itself — are impaired** (§16).
- **Accountability ledger (durable state):** persists signals + admission decisions · **custody state** · `response_case`s · **response obligations (§6)** · **correlation membership + merge/split decisions (§6b)** · typed links · clock values · communications · **closure proof** · audit history. A ledger *stores*; it does not run timers, negotiate handoffs, assemble projections, or dispatch.
- **Shared substrate (~90% composition):** identity · RBAC · consent+purpose · source lineage · evidence timeline (trace_lineage+D7) · relation semantics (§19) · obligations · operator/federation topology · audit · verification. ## §3 — Scope invariant + non-goals

## §5 — Admission (status ≠ disposition; defined dispositions)
- **`admission_status`** (where the decision is): `pending | decided | under_review | appealed`.
- **`admission_disposition`** (what was decided): `admit | admit_provisional | correlate | duplicate | route_out | synchronous_response | suppress | reject`.
- **Defined:** **`admit_provisional`** = the protected auto-admission result (credible allegation/detection of possible safety harm · adverse event · privacy/security breach · mandated-report condition · protected disclosure → a **protected provisional record** bypassing discretionary suppression; final classification may change). **`suppress`** = **retain the signal + the decision durably**, but do NOT open or advance an Accountability matter, under authorized policy + current evidence (appealable; the signal is never discarded). **`reject`** = a corrupt / unauthentic / malformed / abusive / otherwise-invalid submission. **`route_out`** = a *valid* matter owned by another ordinary workflow or external system — **a valid-but-out-of-scope report routes out, it is NOT rejected.** **`duplicate`** = substantially the same reported occurrence/existing record — **but may still carry new evidence AND still owes the reporter a separate communication obligation** (law). **`correlate`** = a *distinct* signal linked to the same suspected matter (§6b).
**Admit when ≥1:** tracked action required · party owed loop-back · duty-to-act/regulatory clock · multiple owners must coordinate · possible meaningful harm/risk/degradation · investigation/verification must persist. *Durable-learning potential MAY strengthen but is NOT independently sufficient.*
**Suppression guardrails (Polaris accountability profile):** AI proposes, never autonomously suppresses a consequential class; records actor·authority·reason·evidence·appeal; implicated party can't self-suppress; protected/anonymous → independent authority.

## §6 — Object model (domain projection ≠ accountability overlay ≠ composed view; obligations defined)
- **Domain `response_projection`** — **published by the domain record** (each canonical in its owning domain): `domain_status` · `domain_classification` · `domain_severity` · `domain_verification_state` · record id+type · owning domain · evidence references. **These are domain-owned; the accountability layer does not set them.**
- **Accountability overlay** — **owned by the Accountability system**: `coordination_status{received|acknowledged|assigned|active|waiting|resolved|verified|closed|reopened}` · `custody_status` · `assignment_status` · `communication_status` · `accountability_closure_status` · escalations. **NOT a mirror of the domain status** — a `patient_safety_event` may remain `domain_status: under_review` while the patient's `response_case` is `closed_with_remedy`; a `service_incident` may be `resolved` while a regulatory response stays open.
- **Composed matter view** — assembles projection + overlay **with field-level source lineage** (§9); what the views (§12) render.
- **`response_obligation`** (candidate cross-cutting semantics — the ledger owns these; do NOT hide them inside a single `committed_remedy`): `obligation_type · owed_to · owner · trigger · due_at/clock · authority_basis · obligation_status · required_proof · waiver_authority · satisfied_by · visibility`. **`obligation_status` is its OWN lifecycle** = `proposed | accepted | active | waiting | satisfied | waived | transferred | breached | overdue | superseded` — **not every obligation is a remedy** (acknowledge/investigate/notify/report have no `remedy_status`). **★ Terminal law:** `authorized_terminal = satisfied | waived | transferred | superseded`; **`breached | overdue` are NONTERMINAL exception states** that escalate and must still reach an authorized terminal — an obligation is **never** "closed" merely because its failure is known (in-progress = `proposed|accepted|active|waiting`). **`remedy_status` applies ONLY to remedial-type obligations** = `proposed | committed | in_progress | delivered | verified | declined | waived_by_authority`. **One `response_case` aggregates several independently-owned obligations** (acknowledge reporter · investigate · notify patient · clinical reassessment · refund · regulatory report · verify correction · offer appeal); **it closes when every required obligation reaches an authorized terminal status + its broader closure conditions are met — the obligation, not a vague case status, is the accountable unit.**
- **`response_case`** — a **native** accountability object: `reported_by · response_owed_to · affected_party · represented_by · responsible_operator · recipient_scope (§7) · visibility_scope · linked_matter · obligations[] · linked_domain_action_records · closure_criteria`. **`committed_remedy` clarified:** the response_case owns the **recipient-facing remedy COMMITMENT + its due/closure state**; the **owning domain owns the actual action** (fulfillment owns replacement · care owns reassessment · commerce owns reimbursement); the response_case **links to those action/obligation records and presents the combined status.** ("We'll replace the med by Tuesday and reassess" = the commitment preserved to the patient; fulfillment/care/commerce execute; Accountability guarantees the combined commitment stays visible and cannot disappear.) Owns no domain truth; optional.
- **Linked domain work (never fused):** REV-184 · OFC exception · D6 dispute · security containment · Platform Change Engineering repair · `problem_investigation` · corrective/preventive action.

## §6b — Correlation truth (persisted) + `matter_graph` (derived)
Correlation is durable decision-making, so it **cannot live only in a derived projection.** Split:
- **Persisted in the ledger:** stable **`matter_id`** · correlation membership · **merge decision · split decision** · decision authority · evidence/rationale · superseded membership · permission metadata.
- **Derived: `matter_graph`** — a projection assembled from those persisted correlation decisions **plus** linked signals · typed domain records · assignments · actions · evidence · `response_case`s. Owns no truth; carries permission-aware projections; preserves original signals. The views (§12) organize around it. (This is not a revived `coordination_case`.)

## §7 — recipient_scope (no unnamed "track")
`recipient_scope = individual | represented_party | cohort | operator | network | regulator`. **A `response_case` may itself be cohort/operator/network/regulator-scoped** — the recipient scope is a property of the response_case, not a separate object. **Individual** response_cases are created only when individualized obligations arise (individualized remedy/evidence/action/communication/appeal/closure). An 80k-patient outage → one cohort-scoped `response_case` for the uniform notice + individual `response_case`s only for those with individualized obligations. If a cohort scope cannot hold needed visibility/remedy semantics, the **obligation model (§6)** carries the cohort scope — no new tracking object is implied.

## §8 — State on its owning object (links are edges)
`signal`:`admission_status`+`admission_disposition` (§5) · **domain projection:`domain_status` (domain-owned)** · **accountability overlay:`coordination_status`+`custody_status`+`assignment_status`+`communication_status`+`accountability_closure_status` (accountability-owned)** · containment:`containment_status{proposed|active|partial|effective|failed|released}` · obligation:`obligation_status` (§6) · remedial obligation / `response_case` remedy:`remedy_status` (§6) · `problem_investigation`:`problem_status{suspected|investigating|known_error|workaround_available|fix_planned|fixed|verified|deferred}` · closure:`closure_disposition{remedy_completed|no_action_required|risk_accepted|transferred|withdrawn}`. Typed links (§19) are edges, never states. **Domain status and coordination status are independent, not mirrored.**

## §9 — Field authority (source-authority law applied here)
Domain record owns classification + lifecycle + severity (its projection). Accountability owns assignment custody · obligations · communication clocks · `response_case` closure (the overlay). Aggregate severity/priority = a **derived projection with source lineage** (every projected field names its authoritative source). **Conflicting classifications preserved + resolved by governed decision, never overwritten** (safety "serious" vs platform "moderate" → both retained).

## §10 — Responsibility graph + assignment + custody laws
Routing = a projection over operator topology · RBAC · capability ownership · Federation grants · jurisdiction · service catalog · on-call · vendor · regulatory → explicit **`assignment`** (lead owner · tracks · resolver · reason · confidence · authority basis · **acceptance status** · deadline · escalation · external handoff · shareable data scope). **Laws:** custody (accountable until an authorized owner *accepts* handoff) · no-orphan (always one custodian + escalation authority + owner-of-last-resort) · routing-conflict (care-continuity→relationship operator · correction→domain owner · shared-platform→OMNI · cross-operator→Network Governance · mandatory-report classification→legally authorized reviewer) · external-delegation (internal owner keeps the clock; vendor silence escalates; vendor closure ≠ OMNI verification; delegation never discharges the obligation) · federation-handoff (authority basis · data-sharing grant · permitted projection · accepted responsibility · deadline · fallback if refused).

## §16 — Intake / protected + out-of-band (with reconciliation authority)
Users never classify the backend; plain-language surfaces; live reporter projection (§12). Protected/non-punitive (confidential+anonymous · whistleblower · non-punitive near-miss · role-separated · sealed notes · anti-retaliation — safe path easier than silence).
**Out-of-band (CNS/normal surfaces — or the coordinator itself — impaired):** store-and-forward capture · minimum offline record (who/what/when/danger?/affected object) · emergency identity verification (step-up for meds/labs/payments) · **authoritative timestamp source** · **offline sequence/idempotency token** · temporary custody owner · tamper evidence · which **mandatory actions may proceed without the primary ledger** (emergency safety escalation, break-glass containment) · fallback if the coordinator is down (external portal/phone/SMS/email · operator emergency contacts · regulator/vendor channels). **Reconciliation authority (the degraded-mode control path) — NO universal winning record** (field/object-specific, applying OMNI's source-authority physics): original signals + evidence stay **append-only, never overwritten** · reporter statements stay reporter-source evidence · **clinical truth** resolves via clinical adoption authority · **custody/assignment** via the ledger · **identity** via Identity assurance · **operational state** via the owning Platform plane · emergency actions stay valid historical events that later link to retrospective authorization / exception review · **conflicting claims stay visible until the owning authority resolves them.** Freshness + authority are *inputs* to reconciliation, not a merge algorithm; who-may-reconcile is scoped per object. ## §17 — Action chain + closure (NOT care closure)
**signal → admission → `impact_assessment` → `containment` → investigation → correction/recovery → *individual remedy/redress* → verification → prevention.** Individual remedy is first-class; a system fix / service restore does NOT discharge it. The response_case **commits the recipient-facing remedy; domains execute the action** (§6). **Closure roll-up — a `response_case` closes ONLY when:** every aggregated **`response_obligation`** reaches an **authorized terminal** state (`satisfied|waived|transferred|superseded` — `breached|overdue` escalate, they do NOT close, §6) · risk contained · remedy delivered · reporting clocks satisfied · reporter communication complete · verification attached · unresolved systemic problems linked+visible · residual risk accepted by a named authority · appeal/reopen rights remain. High-risk → independent verifier. **This doc does NOT define care closure** — care lifecycle is owned by the care domain, may stay longitudinal; outcome evidence links back over time. **Accountability closure = the obligations the concern created are discharged.**

## §18 — Platform ↔ Accountability boundaries + the `response_projection` publication contract
- Ownership + crossing thresholds: pre-release `build_finding` canonical in Platform Change Engineering (PCE) · live `operational_finding`/service condition canonical in Runtime Operations · `product_defect` may link both. Neither becomes an Accountability record unless a **consequence/duty threshold** is crossed.
- **Platform Change Engineering → Accountability:** protected disclosure · compliance issue · material governance violation · already-deployed affected versions · operator notification required.
- **Runtime Operations → Accountability:** patient/provider remedy · operator communication · care-continuity risk · reportable incident · cross-operator responsibility · external/vendor obligation · public/regulator notice.
- **Accountability → Release Operations:** release containment — pause rollout · prevent promotion · recall · halt canary · trigger rollback. **Accountability → Runtime Ops** (live containment) · **Accountability → Platform Change Engineering** (defect investigation + durable repair).
- **★ `response_projection` publication contract (what a domain object publishes on crossing):** a `service_incident` / `product_defect` / deployment failure / runtime finding publishes: record id+type · owning domain · `domain_status` · `domain_classification` · `domain_severity` · affected-object + version references · evidence-capture reference · `domain_verification_state`. The Accountability system then adds its **overlay** (§6) and (if a party is owed) a `response_case`. The finding/defect **stays canonical in its Platform control plane**; the ledger holds the reference + projection + admission. Share: evidence · affected-object refs · severity/urgency · version+trace lineage · ownership · verification links. Do NOT share: lifecycle · canonical records · closure test · visibility · authorities · orchestrator.
