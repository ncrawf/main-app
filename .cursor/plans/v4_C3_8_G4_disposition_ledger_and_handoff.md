## §4 WORKED EXAMPLE — the doctrine in action (proof it's real, not aspirational)
**The 18-yo bodybuilder / TRT override.** Patient wants TRT; labs don't support it; provider wants revenue; OMNI sees the risk; provider overrides. **OMNI-native answer — the mechanism already exists (REV-184 + C3.7 + candidate≠commit + RBAC attestation + `source_authority`), the posture just needs naming:**
- **Patient authority** governs consent/visibility/relationship — *but desire ≠ clinical indication.*
- **Provider/operator authority** may commit within licensure/scope — *but the commitment is attributed, audited, and measured vs standard/evidence* (RBAC attestation + `source_authority`).
- **OMNI substrate** owns the safety envelope + candidate/rationale/contraindication surfacing + audit trace — *AI surfaces risk; AI never commits.*
- **Economic incentive** that would bend care is a **surfaced audit/risk signal, not hidden** (C3.7 economically-blind firewall).
- **REV-184 governed resolution:** the decision resolves to treat / no-treat / defer / monitor / consult / **override-with-attestation** — the override is **allowed under the provider's authority, but legible + attributed + rationale-coded + outcome-tracked.** Non-action-as-commit + disagreement-as-escalation apply.
> OMNI does not block the doctor and does not let low-quality/incentive-skewed judgment **hide.** That is the moat: **legible, attributable, reviewable governed medicine** — not lock-in, not AI-doctor, not conversion-engine. (The Tesla parallel: the machine distinguishes what it *can* do, what it will *autonomously* do, what the human may *manually* choose, what is *logged*, and what *triggers limits* — OMNI does the same for care.)
