### 2. External claim audit of the incumbent submission

This is not nitpicking. Several errors expose where product-name composition is standing in for exact state semantics.

FHIR state models

A FHIR Patient.active field is a boolean—not Tentative → Active. Task and CarePlan also have different status vocabularies; “Draft → Rejected/Accepted” cannot be assigned generically to either. MedicationRequest has its own defined status set.

The larger architectural point is that a native resource must be used for its actual meaning. A collection of nominally related FHIR objects does not automatically form one care-resolution lifecycle.

Stripe history

A PaymentIntent does not transition to a refunded PaymentIntent status. Refund is represented through a separate Refund object and related charge/payment history. That separation is exactly why capture and compensation can both remain historically true.

AuditEvent and proof

FHIR AuditEvent is a security/audit record. The specification does not justify the blanket statement that every API operation, Epic action, or generated draft creates an immutable FHIR AuditEvent. FHIR also distinguishes AuditEvent from Provenance. A complete bounded receipt would therefore require an intentionally assembled proof profile, not merely “we have AuditEvent.”

AI and workflow products

Microsoft’s official Dragon Copilot material supports generation of draft documentation, flowsheet content, and clinician-review workflows. It does not establish a native, cross-product rule that automatically detects the exact demographic/evidentiary/rationale instability in this trace and structurally prevents downstream MedicationRequest creation. That is a plausible custom control, but it must be described as custom.

ServiceNow’s healthcare material emphasizes service operations, nonclinical workflows, and enterprise orchestration; it does not by itself establish a healthcare authority constitution or accepted cross-party custody semantics.

Pharmacy and network semantics

NCPDP SCRIPT supports prescription transactions and change workflows, but a transport or transaction acknowledgment is not automatically acceptance of the patient-level consequence, monitoring obligation, remedy duty, or cross-system proof semantics proposed here. The industry is also transitioning from SCRIPT 2017071 toward SCRIPT 2023011 for the January 1, 2028 compliance date, which reinforces the need to keep the constitutional semantics rail-agnostic.

TEFCA and facilitated FHIR exchange improve exchange capacity. They do not mean an external organization has accepted OMNI’s custody model, consequence model, or portable process receipt.

The incumbent architecture remains plausible. These corrections simply move several claims from “native capability” to “custom healthcare application/profile that must be built and validated.”

### 4. The exact residual

What Gemini has: identity and access control; clinician-only commitment; clinical, payment, and pharmacy records; queueing, timers, SLAs, and escalation; prescription and change-message transport; refunds and payment events; portal status; local audit; manual recovery when a counterparty does not participate. That is substantial.

What Opus specifies that Gemini does not — the distinct function is not “a better case.” It is this composition:
A local rail may terminate while the patient consequence remains unresolved.
Custody does not transfer because a local record says “assigned” or a transport says “received.” It transfers only through authorized acceptance evidence—or remains rejected, expired, released, or unknown.
An exception propagates selectively (jurisdiction → assurance/accountability + remedy; stockout → sourcing/commercial recovery; meaningful formulation/therapy change → clinical reconsideration).
Payment compensation does not erase payment history.
Refund, patient remedy, operational recovery, accountability investigation, clinical reconsideration, and later outcome remain different records.
Patient projection derives from the best-known cross-domain state and cannot continue saying “processing” after known failure.
Proof states what happened, under whose authority, without publishing private deliberation or claiming clinical correctness.
None of this creates one universal source of truth.

The live OMNI estate already contains much of the distributed ownership foundation (one owner per fact; candidate-versus-commit; four-way authority composition; projection-versus-authority; CNS as coordination over commits; OFC separating fulfillment lifecycle from care obligations). The live code, however, currently proves only a smaller slice: a real ten-state treatment-order transition graph; DB-enforced legal transitions; cumulative captured payment amount; status-based patient copy; idempotent outbound messaging. The cross-authority continuity carriers are not production-real yet.

Why R3 rather than R2: the residual becomes most meaningful exactly where OMNI loses direct control (external pharmacy non-ack/refusal/different-formulation; independent payment settlement; EHR owns the record; outside agent; payer coverage under a different authority plane). Why not R4: no independent party has yet accepted the custody semantics, acknowledged a portable receipt, used it to act/settle, integrated the exception taxonomy, recognized the patient-consequence state, reduced a measured cost/harm, paid for the capability, or altered legal/insurance/procurement treatment.
