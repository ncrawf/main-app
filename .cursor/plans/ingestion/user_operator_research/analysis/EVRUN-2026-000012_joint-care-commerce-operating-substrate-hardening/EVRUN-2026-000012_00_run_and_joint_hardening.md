### §0.1 Charter (the actual purpose)

Not "analyze two more screenshot cases." A **bounded, durable architecture-hardening experiment** over a connected three-case cluster to answer:

> **Can OMNI preserve coherent patient AND business consequences while clinical meaning, operator configuration, consent, price, economic incentives, vendor offers, sourcing, payment, fulfillment, and follow-up cross different actors and authorities?** — then harden architecture / product requirements / strategic thesis **only where the evidence earns it.**

First cluster that can seriously test whether **"governed care + business operating substrate" is one integrated architecture or two good ideas sharing a thesis.** Three views of one arc:
- **279** (`EVRUN-2026-000007`, closed / consume-only) — the clinical decision + multi-principal care episode + frozen/unpromoted **OMNI Reactor** candidate.
- **281** (`EVSRC-2026-000281`) — the **supply side**: constructing the service line, catalog, price, consent, multi-vendor network to carry out that care.
- **280** (`EVSRC-2026-000280`) — the **demand side**: a parallel patient request meeting that partially-constructed operating system.

**Decisive product criterion (governing acceptance rule — Knox-corrected):** OMNI must make the operation **materially easier, safer, more reliable, and economically stronger for the operator, while clinical choice remains economically firewalled and incentive-visible.** *Profitability is an operator-level outcome, NOT a clinical-resolution objective.* A pretty patient flow on a worse operating backend = FAIL.

**The hard architecture question (Knox):** not "where does the formulary live," but whether OMNI can keep these meanings **distinct and correctly related** (see §1.5 anti-collapse ledger). The probable gap is **not a new domain** — it is a possibly-inadequately-modeled **relationship family** among: clinical meaning ↔ operator-adopted catalog definition ↔ commercial configuration ↔ counterparty-specific offer ↔ sourcing decision ↔ clinical order ↔ fulfillment order ↔ execution evidence. **The experiment determines this rather than presuming it.**
