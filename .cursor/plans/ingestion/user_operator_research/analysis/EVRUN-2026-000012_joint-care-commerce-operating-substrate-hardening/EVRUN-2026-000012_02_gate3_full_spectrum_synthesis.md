## §15.2 — FINAL CONSTITUTIONAL LAW (activation, not counting)
The Reactor candidate is a **risk-adaptive constitutional TEST. Invariants are NOT counted.** Each lifecycle declares, per invariant: **applicability · activation_condition · profile-specific meaning · enforcement owner · required evidence · failure consequence.** Activation model:
| invariant | activation condition |
|---|---|
| admissibility-before-consequence | evaluated for EVERY consequential transition; depth varies with risk |
| domain-owned commitment | active when authoritative state is committed |
| accepted custody | active when responsibility, work, or unresolved consequence transfers |
| no silent orphaning | active when consequential state remains unresolved |
| selective reopening | active when a later event materially changes an owning lifecycle's semantics |
| compensation ≠ remedy ≠ reconsideration ≠ outcome | active when one or more of those consequences exist |
| honest projection | active whenever state is exposed to a person or system |
| bounded proof | required proportionately for consequential transitions |
**A transition does NOT leave the constitutional family because one invariant has no activation condition in that instance.** A draft spelling-correction may activate neither custody nor remedy; a config rollout affecting ten thousand patients absolutely may. - **Layer 5** — governed matter/consequence projections. **The projection owns no truth, owns no obligations, commits nothing, routes nothing, reopens nothing, remedies nothing.** It EXPOSES linked state · lifecycle states · accepted/unaccepted custody · dependencies · deadlines · unresolved consequences · conflicting closures · uncertainty. **Profile-specific controllers act from that evidence under their own authority — NO universal CNS executive:** CNS where care/business coordination requires it · OFC for fulfillment · Settings/D6/Federation/RBAC owners + a configuration-publication controller for operator config · E&V/Release/Runtime for Platform · Accountability for response · owning domains commit their own state. ## §15.5 — TYPED CUSTODY (three meanings; accepted-custody activates only on a real transfer)
- **change custody** — who carries an approved change from review into effective operation (configuration + Platform release).
- **execution custody** — who accepted responsibility to perform the order/shipment/procedure/task (sourcing + fulfillment).
- **consequence custody** — who currently owns the unresolved patient/operator/financial/response consequence (across Care/OFC/D6/Accountability).
The accepted-custody law applies **only when one of these actually transfers** — it does not force every transaction to invent a custody event.
