### STH-02.2 — The lifecycle is CLEARING & SETTLEMENT, not request/response (beyond Stripe)
A medical action is NOT complete because an API returned `200 OK`. **Six stages** (a longitudinal obligation machine, not a stateless call):
1. **Intent** — someone/something proposes an effect (schedule · obtain identity · order lab · prescribe · change dose · charge · dispense/ship · escalate · consult · administer[physical]). *An intent is never yet authority.*
2. **Clearing** — is the intent *admissible for resolution*? (subject identified · principal · agent delegated · purpose permitted · context fresh/complete · inside pathway · consent required · evidence floor · conflicts/incentives · safety capability present · duplicate/in-progress?). **Clearing ≠ approval — it means the intent is legible + governable enough to enter the action process.**
3. **Commitment** — the owning human + domain make the consequential decision. Model advises · patient prefers · payer authorizes payment · pharmacy confirms supply · operator approves discount — **non-fungible authority planes.** The clinical plan becomes authoritative ONLY through the appropriate clinical committer + owning domain.
4. **Execution** — the authorized action is transmitted to + performed through a **rail** (scheduler · lab · pharmacy · payment · messaging · facility · device · robot · external clinical system). Execution can succeed · fail · partially complete · time out · except · **reveal new facts.**
5. **Settlement & reconciliation** — OMNI determines *what actually happened* (order accepted? specimen collected? med dispensed? delivered to the right patient? appointment attended? robot performed the authorized act? payment settled? did the external system do something DIFFERENT from what was authorized? did only part complete?). **★ Keeper: *Authorized, performed, delivered, and effective are not synonyms.*** Healthcare constantly collapses those states; OMNI cannot.
6. **Continuation / remedy / reopening** — execution creates new obligations (monitor · repeat labs · follow up · notify · reconcile · refund · reverse-where-possible · **mitigate where reversal is impossible** · **reopen the care resolution when an execution exception changes the world model**).
**★ Keeper: *OMNI clears intent, governs commitment, coordinates execution, settles proof, and carries obligations forward.*** The return object is NEVER `success: true` — it carries a **state** from a machine such as: `intent_received · needs_information · inadmissible · pending_consent · pending_human_commit · committed · execution_requested · accepted_by_rail · partially_executed · actualized · externally_reported · reconciled · failed · exception_requires_decision_reopen · remedy_owed · monitoring_obligation_active · outcome_not_yet_known`. **This is the 252 false-closure ladder promoted from peripheral to the actual contract** — it is the practical point at which OMNI stops being middleware.
