Process this one as targeted semantic.

This is not a healthcare strategy source.

It is a technical substrate source for OMNI’s future multimodal architecture.

The useful lesson is that native multimodal AI is not just “LLM plus images.”

The field is actively working through deep representation problems:

how text, image, audio, video, and action become model-readable units,
whether modalities should share parameters or have specialized parameters,
whether understanding and generation use the same representations,
why image/video reasoning does not transfer the same way text reasoning does,
and why physical-world intelligence remains harder than digital multimodal processing.

Core takeaway

Language modeling scaled because text is already a compressed symbolic abstraction of human cognition.

Images, audio, video, and physical-world signals are different.

They are denser, more redundant, more expensive, less abstract, and often require different representations for understanding versus generation.

OMNI translation:

OMNI should not flatten every patient/care signal into “text context.”

A lab, image, voice recording, procedure photo, video, wearable stream, EHR timeline, document, message, and provider note may all need different ingestion, verification, extraction, storage, and action pathways.
