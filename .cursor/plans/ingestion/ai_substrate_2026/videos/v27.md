The record is not replaced. Intelligence runs on top of the record and turns trusted data into action.

Agentic AI shifts this toward systems of intelligent action that reason, plan, and act on top of those records rather than replacing them.

For OMNI, that maps cleanly:

Operator copilot during work
The agent assists in real time using voice, camera, images, procedural guidance, and context. For OMNI, this maps to provider/MA/front-desk/operator copilots.
Multimodal input matters
Voice + camera + clinical images + screenshots + patient messages + labs + telemetry are not separate novelty features. They are future operator context channels.
Closeout is part of the work
The strongest line: “The work isn’t done when the repair is done. It’s done when the record is complete.”

For OMNI, translate that as:

A service, encounter, task, care action, or workflow is not complete until the operational truth, documentation truth, compliance proof, follow-up obligations, and audit trace are complete.
