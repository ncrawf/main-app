1. **The raws are source objects, not doctrine.** They are authoritative only as to what each subagent said. Every architecture question resolves against primary repository sources and the Gate-0/Gate-1b adjudication lineage.
2. **Never edit a preserved body**, and never fill a missing byte from the carrier, a summary, or memory. Each raw carries its own binding Preservation Law.
3. **Preservation is not promotion.** External claims inside these reports — market facts, competitor behaviour, regulatory state, and especially anything forward-looking — do **not** become promotion-grade evidence by sitting in the repository. If OMNI later relies on one, that claim's **primary source** goes through the Evidence Plane router at that time (`D0THES-GRD-036`, capture broad / promotion gated).

5. **A raw's conclusion being later demoted is a reason to keep it, not to discard it.** Lens C is the worked example: its headline contribution was demoted at 0R, and the reasoning behind it survives nowhere else.
