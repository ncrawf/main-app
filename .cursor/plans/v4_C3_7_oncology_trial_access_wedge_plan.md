- **C3.7 question (binding scope):** *Can OMNI become the governed oncology research **access** environment — the front door + multi-sided matching + accrual economy — for patients/families, oncologists, research sites, and pharma sponsors, WITHOUT becoming middleware, corrupting care truth, or laundering pharma accrual incentive through a "care" surface?*

## §2 The new physics C3.7 must pressure (what C3.5/C3.6 did NOT)
1. **Multi-sided marketplace / two-sided network** — supply = open cohort slots; demand = eligible patients. Matching, slot scarcity, geographic feasibility, accrual velocity. (C3.5/C3.6 were single-substrate.)
2. **The accrual incentive economy** — sites are paid by pharma per enrolled patient (per-patient + invoiceable + pass-through fees), atop a coverage-analysis billing grid (research-only vs SOC; double-billing = cardinal sin; PI carries fraud exposure).

## §3 LOAD-BEARING GUARDRAIL (first-class, not a footnote)
**`accrual_incentive ≠ care_recommendation`.** If OMNI is the access environment, accrual pressure and sponsor money MUST be firewalled from the clinical recommendation to a patient/family. This is `payment_care_firewall` (C3.5/D6 §8.1) applied to its sharpest case + **clinical equipoise** + anti-kickback/Stark on the pharma→site payment loop + **double-billing prohibition** (CMS NCD 310.1: each item billed to sponsor XOR insurer, never both). A design that lets enrollment incentive bend the recommendation is **disqualifying** — C3.7 must show OMNI structurally prevents it, or the wedge fails its own ethics test.
- **Sharpened (Opus, broader than the site-only framing): the firewall MUST bind OMNI's OWN economics.** If OMNI is paid by sites/sponsors when a patient enrolls, OMNI has the *same* conflict as the site. The patient-facing recommendation/display engine must therefore be **economically blind** — structurally unable to know or be weighted by who pays OMNI (or how much). This is what separates OMNI from lead-gen-for-pharma. Pressure it in G2; it is the load-bearing trust claim of the whole wedge.
- **Resolved (Nick+Knox 2026-06-14): economically-blind ≠ "OMNI can't earn."** OMNI MAY have a business model with sites/sponsors — SaaS/platform fees, site-ops workflow fees, fixed implementation, sponsor-reporting tools, de-identified feasibility analytics, fair-market-value research-ops support. But **revenue must be structurally firewalled + auditable away from the ranking/display/recommendation path.** Blind-for-recommendation, not no-revenue.
- **Multi-principal neutrality (Opus — the deeper firewall):** OMNI serves **opposing principals on one substrate** — patient (best pathway) vs clinic (retain patient) vs site/sponsor (accrual). Neutrality must be the **architecture, not a policy**: each actor's view is truthful to *that actor's* legitimate scope (operator-neutrality + `knowledge_partition` + firewall), and **no principal's incentive may corrupt another's truth or the patient recommendation.** The sharpest instance is `clinic_retention_incentive ≠ patient_best_pathway` (see §9).

## §3.5 The ACCESS-INTEGRITY plane (Opus — the coherent layer behind Knox patches 2/4/5/6)
The access funnel has **two opposing forces**: the **accrual economy** (sponsor/site payment, slot-filling — pulls toward enrollment) and the **access-integrity plane** (protects the patient from that pull). The §3 firewall is the referee between them. The integrity plane's pressure-candidates (reuse-first; not pre-minted):
- **`trial_option_display_policy` / `match_explanation`** (patch 2) — options ranked/explained by clinical fit · source certainty · eligibility gaps · location/travel feasibility · phase/risk · slot state · patient goals · language/accessibility · **alternative SOC paths** · sponsor/payment-relationship disclosure — **NEVER by who pays.** Likely a CNS Network-Governance policy + the firewall, not a free object.
- **`recruitment_content_gate` / `therapeutic_misconception_guard`** (patch 4) — patient/family copy must distinguish research-from-care, not overpromise benefit, not imply trial=best-treatment, disclose risks+alternatives, use only IRB-approved materials, and keep AI from "selling hope." Likely Messaging 8-gate + IRB-material + D7, not a free object.
- **`candidate_visibility_scope`** (patch 5) — sponsors/sites may NOT see identified pre-screen candidates without consent/legal basis; pre-consent they see aggregate accrual projection / de-identified pipeline / site-level feasibility only. Composes `knowledge_partition` (C3.6) + RBAC + Federation grants + D7 + `source_authority_map`.
- **`trial_support_offer` + anti-inducement gate** (patch 6) — travel/lodging/transport/caregiver/lost-wages/childcare/financial-counseling/sponsor-reimbursement governed so access-support does not become **undue inducement**. D6 + anti-inducement gate + IRB.
- **`cohort_slot_state` / `trial_availability_state`** (patch 3) — registry "recruiting" ≠ truth; pressure actual cohort/site/slot/activation/hold/amendment status, and the **coordinator-says-yes-but-PI-says-not-open** gap. A **freshness/source-authority** problem (who is the authority on "actually open?") — ties to C3.6 `source_authority_map`.
- **`honest_null_terminal`** (Opus) — **"no appropriate trial → SOC is better"** / **"palliative/hospice is the honest answer"** must be a **first-class, non-failure output**, NEVER scored as lost accrual. The deepest anti-funnel guarantee.
- **`standing_match` / longitudinal re-match obligation** (Opus) — "we'll keep watching and tell you if something opens" = a longitudinal `research_navigation_obligation` (substrate-remembers-the-patient; search tools can't). Eligibility is time+evidence-versioned (C3.6 D-008 carry).
- **Equity / equitable-access as first-class** (Opus) — the mission ("Hope Through Access") + FDA diversity mandate make reaching underserved/community populations a *design goal of the display+navigation policy*, not a test row.
- **`cancer_care_navigation`** (Knox patch 3 + Opus) — the patient-facing wedge is NOT "find a trial," it is **"help me understand my cancer-care pathway and whether research belongs in it"**: second opinion · tumor board · SOC options · molecular testing · records upload · insurance/travel feasibility · urgency · goals-of-care · palliative/hospice · local-vs-academic referral · trial-option triage · **honest-null**. This framing is the trust anchor AND what makes the economically-blind engine credible (a navigator naturally outputs "no trial").
- **`trial_supply_feed` / `site_trial_inventory`** (Knox patch 2) — the **supply side**: registry listing + sponsor protocol + site-specific activation + cohort open/closed + slot count + amendment/hold + PI-yes/coordinator-maybe + required-records-before-review + geographic feasibility + can-a-community-clinic-refer-in. Likely resolves to `trial_catalog` + `cohort_slot_state` + P35 ingestion — pressure it explicitly.
- **`screening_yield_funnel` / `candidate_pipeline_state`** (Knox patch 4) — the **high-volume / low-yield reality** (B: 3.4–8.8 hrs/enrolled patient): e.g. 1,000 panel → ~80 pre-screen → ~12 source-backed → ~3 review → 1 enroll + ~20 honest-null + many eligibility-gap obligations. Tests whether OMNI handles low-yield matching **without becoming spam / lead-gen / false hope.** Likely an Operating-Intelligence (`REV-174`) projection.
