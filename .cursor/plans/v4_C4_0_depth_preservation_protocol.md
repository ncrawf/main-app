## Why this exists
The corpus + our reads describe big ideas at varying depth — sometimes the FULL `a→b→c…→m` loop with tensions and implementation consequences, sometimes a boiled-down one-liner. With a large source base, the danger is reading the **boiled-down version once and losing the brilliant full-body articulation** the idea got elsewhere. C3.1's lens row is a **routing map** — it tells you *where* a concept lives, not *how deep* it goes. Writing a section from the lens row alone produces a label ("AI governance," "RAG," "learning loop," "action substrate") instead of the actual architecture.

## Core rule
**No C4 section may be authored from the C3.1 lens row alone.** Before writing a section, pull a compact **Depth Packet** for it — and write from the packet, not the row. (This extends `GRD-044`: don't author from the anchor-ledger receipts; reopen the registry rows + the cited source packets — and pull the *fullest* articulation, not the shortest.)

## The Depth Packet (pull what's relevant — judgment, not a form to fill)
Assemble, for the section's concept(s), as much of the following as actually exists. **Not every field applies to every section** — a thin/vocabulary lens needs little; a load-bearing lens (loops, AI-substrate, reservoirs/learning, action-substrate, federation, eval) needs most of it:
- the **C3.1 lens row(s)** (the routing pointer);
- the **RICHEST prior articulation(s)** of the idea — the fullest version anywhere (registry §1 cluster + the **per-source §3 Review-003** + ORIENTATION §1 verbatim + the fuller v2/v3 spine passage), **not** the shortest summary;
- relevant **EVRUN-000002 registry rows / §2A / §3T tension-register** anchors;
- relevant **per-source Review-003** anchors (where the depth actually lives);
- relevant **ORIENTATION §2 / strategic-read** passages;
- relevant **v2/v3 doctrine · contracts · planes · guardrails**;
- the **full mechanism / loop / sequence** when one exists (the whole `a→…→m`, not "the loop");
- **flattening risk** — what is lost if reduced to a generic phrase;
- **thesis obligation** — what the v4 spine must preserve (timelessly);
- **deferrals** — what belongs to C3.5 / C5 / contracts / primitive registry / schemas / open-review.

## How to find the richest version (the mechanic)
Registry §1 row → its `downstream homes` + the cited source ids → **reopen those sources' §3 Review-003 + the ORIENTATION/verbatim passage** → take the fullest articulation found. The registry is the index; the depth lives in the §3 reads and the verbatim. If two articulations conflict in depth, the **fuller one is the source of truth for the idea**; the boiled-down one is a summary, not the concept.

## Anti-flattening examples (Knox)
- Not "AI governance" → pull the AI-substrate / runtime / capability-envelope / context-routing / output-authority / eval lineage.
- Not "RAG / knowledge" → pull retrieval≠authority, clinical-doc RAG, vector/hybrid/freshness/pruning, D7/Observation/Clinical-Memory, context modes, provenance.
- Not "learning loop" → pull provider/staff/inventory/business/security/connector/build-agent learning, reservoir families, gates, no-silent-policy-mutation.
- Not "action substrate" → pull the full Sense → Decide → Act → Prove/Learn chain and show action as ONE mechanism inside the execution substrate.

## Acceptance rule (binding on the C4 spine gate)
**A C4 section FAILS review if it uses the right lens name but loses the depth, mechanism, tension, or ownership law behind it.** (Reviewer test: "name the lens" is not enough — point to the mechanism/loop/tension/ownership-law the section preserves.) This composes with the C3.1 acceptance criterion (controlling plan C4 (h)).

## Anti-rigidity (Nick 2026-06-13 — do NOT let this become bureaucracy)
- **Proportional:** the Depth Packet is required where there IS depth (load-bearing lenses); a genuinely thin/vocabulary lens needs only a line. Use judgment about packet weight per section — don't file a heavyweight packet for a trivial one.
- **Spirit, not verbatim plucking:** the goal is to carry the **full-body read** of an idea into timeless prose — NOT to copy-paste quote fragments. (Quotes are worktable evidence; the thesis states the idea in its own voice — and timelessly, per the C3/C4 rule.)
- **Worktable, not an archive deliverable:** Depth Packets are authoring scratch (live in the reconciliation ledger / section worktable); they do not need to be preserved as standalone canonical files unless useful.
- **The checklist guides, it doesn't gate every field:** Knox's field list is a prompt to look in the right places, not a form whose every box must be ticked.
