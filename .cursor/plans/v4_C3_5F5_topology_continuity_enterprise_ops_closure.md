## P35 RENAME (Nick/Knox 2026-06-14 — corrects F4's over-crystallization)
F4 named P35 "Device-Link / Telemetry-Ownership." That was **too device-flavored and baked in unknown mechanics.** **P35 is renamed/generalized and this is the authoritative version (supersedes F4's framing; F4 rows stand, the family name updates):**
- **P35 — External Capability / Signal-Command Boundary.** For ANY external system OMNI connects to (watch, vent, HR monitor, Tesla surgical robot, lab analyzer, IV pump, phone/PBX, cafeteria system, camera, bed sensor, API, MCP tool, future device), OMNI governs the **integration contract, provenance, permission, authority, proof, audit, and action loop** — and assumes a **different relationship to each** via an explicit **capability mode**:
  - `read-only` · `write-back` · `request-only` · `bounded-command` · `human-confirmed-command` · `prohibited-command` · `emergency-break-glass` · `vendor-operated`.
- **OMNI does not assume it owns the mechanics or the native UI.** A watch we read; a hospital HR monitor we may own the surface/revenue; a robot is vendor-operated; a vent may be `read-only` for tidal-volume but `bounded-command` for rate. The mode is a per-system, per-context governed decision — **not a thesis assumption.** (`command_authority_boundary` from F4 = the per-mode object; `external_capability` = the family.)
