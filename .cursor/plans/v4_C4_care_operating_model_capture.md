## §1 — Purpose, boundary, authority
**Care Operating Model = OMNI's longitudinal care engine: Sense + Planning/Commitment + Act/Fulfillment loops, governed-resolution + authority gates, and recurrent Prove/Learn feedback across signal, resolution, encounter, order, obligation, episode, relationship, longitudinal-context, and outcome horizons.** It is **an umbrella over domain-owned truth**, NOT a new truth-owning domain and NOT one executive. Truth stays canonical in CM/Observation/D5/OFC/D3/D7/D6.

## §2 — Nested temporal topology (rejects "Care never closes")
**[INV] Care is longitudinal because completed acts, outcomes, and unresolved obligations RE-ENTER sensing — NOT because nothing closes.** Objects have their own terminal / dormant / transferred / superseded / reopened states across nested horizons: **signal** (expire/supersede) · **resolution** (REV-184 stance; reopenable) · **encounter** (opens→closes — *state owned per §14/§15, rendered by D5 `encounter_view`*) · **order/fulfillment** (`fulfillment_order`) · **obligation** (`care_obligation`; incl. `expired_unfulfilled`, §15) · **episode** (`care_episode` — concurrent episodes **independently stateful but may be clinically related**) · **relationship** (`treating_owner` transferable) · **longitudinal patient context** (persists) · **outcome/population-learning** (long-latency; may reopen a resolution). *(care_commitment lifecycle + "ownership closes care" are `[OPEN]`, moved to §8 — not carried as settled.)*

## §3 — Sense loop (what is true about this patient now, by whom, at what authority) — BRANCHED, not one chain
Not every source produces a media artifact; extraction precedes derived observation for unstructured media; **observation ≠ clinical assertion** (different truth planes).
```
source_event
├─ direct structured source (lab feed · device reading · appointment/dispense event · structured intake)
│     └─ observation / operational_event            (Observation / owning domain)
├─ media or document (unstructured)
│     └─ extraction_run  →  extracted observation candidate  +  extracted assertion candidate
├─ external committed record (other operator / prior system)
│     └─ attributed_external_state                  (source-authority labeled)
└─ patient / provider statement
      └─ source_attributed_claim                    (Intake §7 — never auto-adopted)
        ↓
 integrity (D7) · data-fidelity (Obs) · source-authority evaluation
        ↓  verification where applicable
 clinical adoption ONLY where clinical MEANING is asserted (Clinical Memory; provider is the adopter)
        ↓  contradiction / supersession handling (append-only)
 authority-labeled context projection (context_packet — MAY include non-adopted evidence, each item labeled)
```
- **[INV] Observation ≠ assertion (distinct planes):** a measured/reported fact (`BP 158/96` · `med dispensed` · `patient-reported nausea` · `no-show` · `low sleep duration`) is an **observation**; **clinical meaning** (`uncontrolled hypertension` · `suspected adverse effect` · `adherence concern` · `deterioration`) is a later **clinical_assertion** the provider adopts. Do not collapse them.
- **[INV] Context may include non-adopted evidence** — a governed `context_packet` can carry raw/verified observation · patient-source claim · externally-committed state · **unadopted candidate** · adopted assertion · **unresolved contradiction**, *provided every item is authority-labeled*. It does NOT require clinical adoption to enter context (only to become clinical truth / drive clinical action).
- **[INV] AI/automation boundary (corrected — the absolute "AI never satisfies a gate" was wrong):** automated systems **may perform or support bounded integrity / fidelity / classification / technical-verification** where the **owning contract permits** — document-integrity validation, extraction confidence, source-feed validation, schema conformance, duplicate/anomaly detection, image-quality, deterministic threshold checks; a deterministic service **may commit a technical verification state under policy**. **AI NEVER** performs clinical adoption, silently converts evidence into clinical truth, or acquires clinical authority merely by passing a technical check.
- **LI signal ladder (LIMITED-USE):** `raw_signal → normalized_observation → trend → contextual_interpretation → CNS_candidate → action/no_op/review/escalation → optional D7 materialization`; **baseline+delta** (raw thresholds insufficient); **absence-of-signal is a context state, not proof of normality**; LI informs timing/routing/prioritization/suppression — **never commits** (LI §4). *Re-verify vs Tier-0 before binding.*
- **Sharpenings (durable pointers, §19):** context-assembly = governed mixture (freshness/authority/budget; length≠authority) · anchor-relative context (Δ from anchor events) · PHI stays external/cited, never weight-baked · reject eventual-consistency for action-critical clinical knowledge.

## §6 — Act / Fulfillment loop (what was requested, and did it get done) — BRANCHED
Not every act creates a D5 service occurrence. Branches:
```
authorized intent
├─ direct domain commit            (adopt assertion · update med plan · record no-action decision)
├─ planned work                    (D3 appointment · procedure series)
├─ internal service occurrence     (D5 — actualized work)
├─ external fulfillment            (external pharmacy fill · outside lab · vendor)
├─ communication                   (Messaging — reply · instruction · result release)
├─ monitoring                      (care_obligation: recall/retest/refill/watch)
└─ no-action / defer               (a committed resolution, §4 — not "nothing")
```
- **[INV] Ownership (contracts):** OFC owns `fulfillment_order` (`requested→authorized→ordered→in_fulfillment→output_received→reviewed→released→completed` + `cancelled/expired/exception`) + `care_obligation` — **state only, truth delegated** ("ledger of the release, not the decider", OFC §6). D5 owns `service_occurrence`+`care_episode` (completion ≠ closure). D3 = planning only (booked ≠ performed ≠ consented ≠ charted).
- **[INV] Appointment is ONE possible materialization of a `care_obligation` — NOT always its product, and NOT always from an obligation.** Appointments also originate from authorized intent independently: patient request · clinician plan · standing protocol · preventive scheduling · procedure series · operator workflow · reschedule · new-care intake.
- **[INV] `fulfillment_order` and `care_obligation` are a SEPARABLE family** (thesis §8.6): commerce = fulfillment without obligation; monitoring/recall = obligation without external order.
- **[INV] Fulfillment exception reopens *clinical* resolution ONLY when the clinical picture changes** — clinical feasibility changes · delay changes risk · a substitute changes the care decision · patient preference/consent changes · the original plan is no longer viable. A pharmacy stockout that resolves via alternate location / equivalent supplier / delayed fill / insurer routing is an **operational** reroute, NOT a clinical reopen. (Corrects the earlier over-routing.)
- **[INV] False-closure ladder (C3.5 / EVRUN-000004):** `planned ≠ authorized ≠ transmitted ≠ in-stock ≠ dispensed ≠ obtained` — order transmission ≠ patient got care.
- **[OPEN] `administration_event` semantic split (do NOT let a fulfillment ledger own clinical occurrence truth):** OFC may own the **administration workflow state**; **D5 (or a clinical administration owner) owns what actually occurred** (given/held/partial/interrupted/refused/wasted/dose-changed/adverse-response); **Observation/CM own the resulting patient facts + meaning**; **D7** owns signed evidence. Decide the split before assigning the owner.
- **Clinical-action ladder (video, §19):** education → suggestion → evidence-candidate → review → **authorized-commit → fulfillment → monitoring**; anti-rubber-stamp (review must be meaningful).

## §7 — Prove / Outcome / Learn / Re-enter Sense (was scattered — now first-class)
Every committed Act must emit an observable effect-signal that closes back into Sense (video 186). Architected dimensions: **expected effect · observed effect · patient-reported outcome · adverse outcome · no-observable-outcome · causal uncertainty · confounders · time horizon · follow-up completion · recurrence · treatment failure · correction · learning promotion · population-vs-patient-specific inference.**
- **[INV] Decision-quality ≠ realized-outcome (the deepest care law here; REV-184-aligned, video 265):** *an outcome may change future decisions without proving the earlier decision was negligent, wrong, or irrational under the context available at the time.* Outcome reads the frozen context (§4); it never retroactively rewrites the reasonableness of the prior decision.
- **[INV] Learning routes by kind, gated:** care-outcome learning → Outcome-Intelligence/RWE (REV-174 sub-plane) + owning care domains; **never silently mutates clinical policy** (LI §9 outcome states: ignored/acknowledged/resolved/escalated/overridden/clinically_acted_on/suppressed/false_positive → improves ranking/suppression). Population inference stays de-identified + governed.

## §8 — Longitudinal coherence ("care that remembers you") — expanded
**[INV] Distinguish the continuity types (each has its own owner + rules):** identity · source · clinical-state · decision · plan · relationship · responsibility · obligation · episode · communication · fulfillment · outcome · **cross-operator** continuity (`continuity_binding` = identity + grants + `care_episode` + modality; **visibility ≠ authority** across settings). **State classes a longitudinal item can hold:** `current · historical · provisional · contradicted · superseded · disputed · dormant · no-longer-relevant · indefinitely-clinically-significant`. `longitudinal_projection` = **hard projection** (derived substrate, own lifecycle) — *projection ≠ truth*; temporary composed context is governed, not automatic canonical truth (LI §10.1). Assertion carry-forward (FIELD-002) must be **provenance-preserving** ("carried from d/c 3 notes ago, never re-verified") — never blind copy-forward; the note is a materialized `encounter_view`; assertions have independent longitudinal life (adopt/supersede).

## §9 — Actors + care topology — FOUR separated layers (roles NOT locked from an abandoned thesis)
Distinguish four things (do not conflate):
1. **Human clinical-accountability role:** `treating_owner` · `ordering_clinician` · `supervising_clinician`.
2. **Organizational responsibility:** operator · department · care team · coverage pool · service line.
3. **Operational custody:** queue · on-call role · current work owner · backup owner.
4. **Execution actor:** human · protocol · deterministic system · AI agent · external system · device.
- **[ARCH] The thesis §7.2 six-role set** (`treating_owner`/`consulting_provider`/`ordering`/`supervising`/`monitoring_owner`/`open_loop_owner`) is a **strong candidate** (DEEP-HISTORICAL), NOT a locked `[INV]`; `open_loop_owner` may be a named human OR an accountable role OR a team-with-accepted-custodian+fallback, not one static human at every instant.
- **[INV] Non-human actors** may participate in deliberative workflow as **bounded candidate/evidence producers** (generate candidates · identify disagreement · compare options · simulate · retrieve evidence · record rationale). They may **NOT** hold the human/organizational care-accountability role, vote themselves into consensus, or become the clinical author merely by participating.
- `care_team_graph` = patient-/purpose-/consent-scoped graph, NOT an org chart. Family/surrogate: `surrogate_authority → visibility_grant → conversation_scope → rail → update-obligation` (C3.5 Chain C); **delegated ≠ inherited authority**.

## §10 — CNS: positive definition + hard limits
**[INV] CNS is the governed care-coordination + resolution control plane.** It **coordinates**: authorized-context assembly · candidate creation + routing · resolution + orchestration lineage · **care-owner ROUTING + accepted-assignment coordination** (propose · route · request acceptance · record accepted assignment · escalate non-acceptance — it does **NOT silently assign** treating ownership unless an authoritative deterministic policy explicitly permits that transition) · **orchestration-request initiation** (CNS initiates a candidate/orchestration request; **the owning domain authorizes + commits** the actual order/fulfillment transition) · follow-up · exception routing · cross-domain + cross-operator care transitions · longitudinal reconsideration. Maintains the **`clinical_required` permit-gate** (cleared only by patient inbound / staff resolution / deterministic policy — **never AI**, CNS §10.1).
- **[INV] CNS does NOT own:** clinical truth · observation truth · appointment truth · occurrence truth · fulfillment truth · payment truth · signed documents · clinical authorship · every care obligation · every native domain lifecycle. Owns **process-state, not truth** (candidate → resolver/policy → **owning-domain commit**). Not sovereign, no mini-brain sprawl (CNS §6). Cross-operator default = **passive coherence** (Patient-CNS observes+signals, never delivers care); active coordination needs an explicit `care_coordination_owner`.
