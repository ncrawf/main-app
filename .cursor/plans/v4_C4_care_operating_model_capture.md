## §1 — Purpose, boundary, authority
**Care Operating Model = OMNI's longitudinal care engine: interlocking Sense + Act/Fulfillment loops, governed-resolution + authority gates, and recurrent Prove/Learn feedback across patient, relationship, episode, obligation, and outcome horizons.** It is **an umbrella over domain-owned truth**, NOT a new truth-owning domain and NOT one executive. **[INV] Non-goals:** it does not own clinical truth (CM/Observation), occurrence truth (D5), fulfillment truth (OFC), appointment truth (D3), documents (D7), payment (D6), or clinical authorship — those stay canonical in their contracts. It composes them into governed motion.

## §2 — Nested temporal topology (rejects "Care never closes")
**[INV] Care is longitudinal because completed acts, outcomes, and unresolved obligations RE-ENTER sensing and may change later decisions — NOT because nothing closes.** Individual care objects have their own terminal / dormant / transferred / superseded / reopened states. The model carries **nested horizons**, each with its own closure semantics:
- **signal** (captured, may expire/supersede) · **resolution** (REV-184 stance reached; reopenable) · **encounter** (opens→closes; a D5 `encounter_view`) · **order/fulfillment** (`fulfillment_order` requested→…→completed/cancelled/expired/exception) · **obligation** (`care_obligation` due→fulfilled/superseded/expired_unfulfilled) · **episode** (`care_episode` longitudinal thread; concurrent episodes independent) · **relationship** (`treating_owner` transferable; care lane long-running) · **longitudinal patient context** (persists; never one final closure while the relationship continues) · **outcome / population-learning** (long-latency; may reopen an earlier resolution).
**[INV]** `care_commitment` lifecycle carries `completed | expired | reopened | scope_changed` (thesis §7.3); a case resolves preserving dissent, `re_opened` on new evidence. Ownership doctrine (§7.3): *"Choice routes care. Assignment records care. Commitment creates accountability. Ownership closes care"* — ownership is scoped, temporal, transferable, auditable.

## §3 — Sense loop (what is true about this patient now, by whom, at what authority)
**Chain (contract-grounded):** `source_event → media_artifact (D7) → observation (Observation) → extraction → extracted/candidate assertion → verification + clinical adoption (CM, provider-only) → committed assertion → authorized context_packet (CNS, non-canonical)`.
- **[INV] Three independent gates never collapse** (contracts): **artifact-integrity** (D7) ≠ **data-fidelity** (`source_feed_verified`/`human_qc_verified`, Observation §4) ≠ **clinical-adoption** (`clinical_adoption_state`, CM §8 — provider is the only adopter; **AI never confirms, never satisfies a gate**).
- **Patient-source** (Intake §7): intake/patient-generated evidence **NEVER auto-promotes to clinical truth** — stays `not_adopted` until a provider adopts (`SC-INTK-CM-001`).
- **[INV] LI signal ladder (LIMITED-USE)**: `raw_signal → normalized_observation → trend → contextual_interpretation → CNS_candidate → action/no_op/review/escalation → optional D7 materialization`. **Baseline+delta** (raw thresholds insufficient); **absence-of-signal is a context state, not proof of normality**; LI may inform timing/routing/prioritization/suppression/candidate-quality but **may not create D5/commerce/D7 rows without owning-domain rules** (LI §4). *Authority: limited-use; re-verify vs Tier-0 + contracts before treating as binding.*
- **Sharpenings (video, `SHARPENS-CARE`):** context-assembly = governed mixture (authority·relevance·**freshness**·risk·budget·dedupe — length≠authority, 164); **anchor-relative** care context (Δ from anchor events, not a flat pile, 165); PHI/authoritative facts stay **external/cited, never weight-baked** (243); reject eventual-consistency for action-critical clinical knowledge (262).

## §4 — Governed-resolution gate (REV-184's home — at the gate, NOT generalized)
The **Decide layer between Sense and Act** (thesis §8 authority gate). REV-184 §0 supplies: `world_model` (partial, time-stamped; **predicted_state = candidate, not truth**) · `trust_horizon` (authorized-autonomy ≤ trusted-horizon; shortened by staleness/uncertainty/irreversibility/blast-radius) · stances (`act · no-act/not-indicated · defer · monitor · consult · dispute · decline · preserve-option · escalate-if-fails · uncertain · emergency-first · post-hoc-reconciliation`) · two axes (`gate_holder_posture` × `world_model_trust`) · **non-action is a committed decision** · disagreement = escalation · **outcome reads the frozen context, never rewrites it** · reopen/supersede.
- **Ownership (REV-184 §0.4, do NOT collapse):** CNS owns resolution **record/lifecycle/graph** (process-state); RBAC owns the **authority/blast-radius/reversibility gate**; CM owns **truth + commit**; OFC owns **obligations from a stance**; D5 = occurrence; D7 + `trace_lineage` = proof.
- **[INV] Do NOT generalize REV-184 into the lifecycle of every care object.** It governs the *decision/resolution*, not the `care_episode`, `fulfillment_order`, or relationship lifecycles (those are §5/§2).
- **[INV] Recommendation-integrity firewall (C3.7):** accrual/revenue/retention pressure **must not bend what the patient sees as clinically appropriate** — a structural, auditable, economically-blind presentation invariant (posture-invariant across clinic/sponsor/academic/OMNI-economics). **honest-null terminal** (no appropriate option / SOC-better / not-eligible-yet→standing-match) = a first-class committed decision, **never a failed conversion**.

## §5 — Act / Fulfillment loop (what was requested, and did it get done)
**Chain:** `intent/request → eligibility/consent/authority → commitment/order → fulfillment → service occurrence (D5) → output/result → review/release → follow-up/retest/refill/exception → re-entry into Sense`.
- **[INV] Ownership (contracts):** OFC owns `fulfillment_order` (`requested→authorized→ordered→in_fulfillment→output_received→reviewed→released→completed` + `cancelled/expired/exception`) and `care_obligation` — **state only, all truth delegated** (OFC §3, §6 "ledger of the release, not the decider"). D5 owns `service_occurrence` + `care_episode` (completion ≠ closure; does NOT imply D6 settlement or D7 documentation). **D3 = planning only** (booked ≠ performed ≠ consented ≠ charted); appointment is a **conversion target of `care_obligation`**, not the Act carrier.
- **[INV] `fulfillment_order` and `care_obligation` are a SEPARABLE family** (thesis §8.6): commerce = fulfillment without obligation; monitoring/recall = obligation without external order.
- **[INV] False-closure ladder (C3.5/FIELD-004):** `planned ≠ authorized ≠ transmitted ≠ in-stock ≠ dispensed ≠ obtained` — a system fix / order transmission does not prove the patient got care; execution-exception (pharmacy stockout) **reopens the clinical resolution**, not just a fulfillment reroute.
- **Inpatient physics (C3.5, `net-new`/`extension`):** `movement_state` (ADT/bed/LOC/attending-of-record; authority **recomputes on transfer**) → D5; `administration_event` (order ≠ administration; held/partial/interrupted/waste) → OFC — the highest-leverage act-loop gap.
- **Clinical-action ladder (video 149, `GENUINELY-NEW-FOR-CARE`):** education → suggestion → evidence-candidate → review → **authorized-commit → fulfillment → monitoring** as one ladder; anti-rubber-stamp (review must be meaningful).

## §6 — Longitudinal coherence ("care that remembers you")
Patient identity through time · current vs historical state · active/prior care relationships + `treating_owner` transfer (explicit, audited) · goals/preferences · episodes + continuation triggers · pending/breached obligations · monitoring · recurrence + **absence-of-expected-signal** · dormant vs closed · cross-operator continuity (`continuity_binding` = identity + grants + `care_episode` + modality; **visibility ≠ authority** across settings). **[INV]** `longitudinal_projection` is a **hard projection** (derived substrate, own lifecycle) — *projection ≠ truth*; temporary composed context is a governed temporary arrangement, not automatic canonical truth (LI §10.1). Assertion carry-forward (FIELD-002) must be **provenance-preserving** ("carried from d/c 3 notes ago, never re-verified") — never blind copy-forward; the note is a materialized `encounter_view`, assertions have independent longitudinal life (adopt/supersede).

## §7 — Actors + care topology
**6 HUMAN-only roles** (thesis §7.2; AI/device/external-system NEVER occupy): `treating_owner` (commits plan; one per care lane at a time) · `consulting_provider` (bounded opinion only) · `ordering_provider` · `supervising_provider` · `monitoring_owner` · `open_loop_owner` (next unresolved step; rotates, audited). Plus patient · caregiver/surrogate (`surrogate_authority`; **delegated ≠ inherited authority**) · operator · Federation · external system · **AI candidate-generator** · payer · vendor. **`care_team_graph`** = patient-/purpose-/consent-scoped graph, **not an org chart**. **[INV] Multi-actor = multi-HUMAN-actor**: AI contributes candidates/summary/`disagreement_signal`, never a deliberating actor; NOT a marketplace of opinions, NOT equal-authority free-for-all, NOT AI-mediated consensus (thesis §8.5). Family/care-team access = `surrogate_authority → visibility_grant → conversation_scope → rail → update-obligation` (C3.5 Chain C).

## §8 — CNS: positive definition + hard limits
**[INV] CNS is the governed care-coordination + resolution control plane.** It **coordinates**: authorized-context assembly · candidate creation + routing · resolution + orchestration lineage · care-owner assignment · fulfillment initiation · follow-up · exception routing · cross-domain + cross-operator care transitions · longitudinal reconsideration. It maintains the **`clinical_required` permit-gate** (a message can block a prescribe/continuation permit; cleared only by patient inbound / staff resolution / deterministic policy — **never by AI**, CNS §10.1).
**[INV] CNS does NOT own:** clinical truth · observation truth · appointment truth · occurrence truth · fulfillment truth · payment truth · signed documents · clinical authorship · every care obligation · every native domain lifecycle. It owns **process-state, not truth** (candidate → resolver/policy → **owning-domain commit**). **CNS is not the Care executive and not sovereign** — no mini-brain sprawl ("Messaging CNS / Intake CNS" rejected, CNS §6). Cross-operator default = **passive coherence** (Patient-CNS observes + signals, never delivers care); **active coordination** requires an explicit `care_coordination_owner`. (This closes the `FWREG-007` "CNS central but undefined" gap positively.)

## §9 — Polaris / authority composition (Clinical profile family)
**[INV] The Clinical profile is a named FAMILY of alignment concerns + authoritative owners** for a care transition class; the **applied posture is computed** per `actor × relationship × patient × purpose × evidence × consent × jurisdiction × risk × timing × reversibility × current-authority` (C4.1 computed-projection — NOT a hardcoded mode). REV-184 lives **only** in the Clinical profile's resolution semantics. Authority-by-action-class (CNS §90): low-authority evidence is fine for routing/prioritization/suppression/review-candidate; **clinical ACTION requires provider adoption / owning-domain commit / policy gate**.

## §10 — Care health (must be AT LEAST as legible as Platform + Accountability health)
**[INV] A care operating model must expose care-health, or Platform/Accountability health will be more observable than the thing OMNI exists for.** Signals (composed projection, field-level authority — NOT co-owned truth): signal-capture latency · unreviewed-candidate age · context freshness · unresolved contradiction · decision latency · **result-not-communicated** · obligation at-risk/breached · fulfillment completion · follow-up completion · **lost-to-follow-up** · care-owner continuity · handoff acceptance · provider-queue burden · **patient burden / alert fatigue** (HCI failures ARE care failures; `capacity_aware_action_threshold`, video 155/183) · outcome visibility · recurrence. Attention is routed by interrupt tiers `{auto-no-op · draft · must-decide · urgent}` under an `ambient_suppression_policy` (video 086/062) — scarce human attention is routed, not just tasks. **Gap noted:** no carrier mints **deterministic care timers** (follow-up SLA, med-recall/retest windows) as a named primitive — only loop/cron (089) + obligation hooks (157) gesture; **[C5] owed.**

## §11 — Degraded / out-of-band care
`emergency-first` · missing context · unavailable provider · unavailable system · external intervention · retrospective reconciliation · break-glass · **safe-halt vs safe-continuation**. Downtime is a **first-class operating state** (C3.5 P16): out-of-band care → recovery → back-entry reconcile (author + timestamp + authority; append-only, never overwrite). Emergency actions are valid historical events that later link to retrospective authorization (REV-184 `emergency-first`, `post-hoc-reconciliation`). Assume every component (incl. monitors) can fail (video 264); `instrumentation_health_state ∈ {active|degraded|absent}` — the system must know whether required care observability is live.

## §12 — Cross-loop seams (typed; per the shared seam model in the sibling captures)
- **Care → Platform** *(evidence_publication: learning)* — care-outcome/effect evidence → Platform E&V + Product/Outcome-Intelligence (safe-but-ineffective capability, poor retrieval, ignored-model-no-harm) — **NOT via Accountability** unless harm/duty/remedy/disclosure.
- **Platform → Care** *(consequence_notification)* — a live platform defect may have affected a clinical decision/order/communication/fulfillment/`care_obligation` (Platform §0a trace).
- **Care → Accountability** *(consequence_notification)* — safety event · disputed care decision · missed obligation · harm.
- **Accountability → Care** *(work_trigger/reopen)* — reopen a REV-184 resolution; Accountability keeps its response obligations.
- **Care → Agent-Runtime** — a care-facing agent runs under Polaris + REV-184 (retrieve/explain/draft/propose/execute-bounded; never silently commits clinical truth); the runtime/harness is homed in `v4_C4_agent_runtime_and_harness_capture.md`.

- **Care ↔ Federation / external capabilities** — external-capability 8-modes (C3.5 P35: read-only … human-confirmed-command … prohibited … break-glass … vendor-operated); OMNI owns loop+data+record+command/authority+proof; device/vendor owns physical actuation.
