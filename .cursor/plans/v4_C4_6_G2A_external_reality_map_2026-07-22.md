## §2 — G2-A · Standards + incumbent capability (STEELMAN)

**Rule for this stream (Knox):** for every claimed gap, state *what already exists · what is technically possible via configuration · what is actually deployed · what crosses organizations · what is institutionally relied upon · what remains only local state / message exchange* — before claiming any residual. No incumbent strawmen.

### §2.1 — NCPDP SCRIPT + Surescripts (the real US prescribing rail)
The single most important steelman: **incumbent rails already provide acceptance rungs 1–4 at genuine cross-org, contractually-relied scale.** OMNI must claim zero credit for these.

| Capability | Highest honest tier | Evidence (`as_of 2026-07-22`) |
|---|---|---|
| E-prescription routing (`NewRx`) | **T5** contractual reliance | Surescripts 2024: **2.6B e-prescriptions**, 27.2B total transactions; ~1.34M active e-prescribers. NCPDP SCRIPT is the mandated Part D standard. |
| Prescriber↔pharmacy **negotiation loop** (`RxChangeRequest/Response`: Approved / ApprovedWithChanges / Denied / Validated; `RxRenewalRequest/Response`) | **T4** cross-org | A *real structured accept / approve-with-changes / deny state exchange across org boundaries* — not just transport. This is the incumbent analog of "typed A2A work." |
| `CancelRx` / `CancelRxResponse`; `Status` / `Verify` / `Error` (ACK/receipt/fault) | **T3–T4** | Production; the ACK/receipt layer = rungs 2–3. |
| Medication history (`RxHistoryRequest/Response`) | **T4** | 3.31B responses (2024). |
| Real-Time Prescription Benefit (RTPB) | **T4**, partial **T6** | ~889,907 prescribers; **788M responses** (2024); partial third-party outcome evidence (Desai et al., *JAMA Intern Med* 2022). |
| Fill/dispense closed loop (`RxFill`: dispensed / partial / not-dispensed) | **T2**, patchy **T3** | The *canonical* dispense-status message — but **historically low adoption**, prescriber-opt-out, NIST-noted barriers. **This is the seam where the "closed loop" breaks in the real world.** |
| Structured & Codified Sig | **T2**, ~T3 | ~10% structured use (2020); "47% of free-text could have been structured." |
| Electronic prior authorization (ePA transaction set) | **T2** → mandate arriving | Becoming *mandatory in certified EHRs by 2027-12-31* (ONC HTI-4). |

**Version/mandate timeline (hard dated `as_of` facts — feed C4.5 temporal discipline):**
- SCRIPT **2017071** exclusive since 2020-01-01; **CMS-4205-F2** (final 2024-06-17) sets **2023011 mandatory/exclusive 2028-01-01**, 2017071 **sunsets 2027-12-31** (transition period allows either until then).
- **F&B v60** (plan-level batch formulary) + **RTPB v13** (patient-level real-time benefit) **mandatory 2027-01-01**.
- **2023011 adds a dedicated compound section, REMS elements, e-controlled-substance transfers, formalized ePA** — i.e., the standard *is* moving toward structured compounds, **but not required until 2028**.

**Steelman verdict:** the rails genuinely solve *transport + schema + ACK + accepted-for-review + a real change/renewal negotiation loop + medication history + real-time benefit* at T4–T5. What they do **not** natively carry: cross-authority *execution-custody* semantics, patient-consequence continuity under partial failure, portable admissibility, and a *structured, adopted* fill/dispense closed loop (RxFill is T2/patchy-T3). Rungs 1–4 = incumbent. Rung 5+ = open.

### §2.5 — Palantir Foundry + AIP (accurate steelman — Knox Patch 9 / §12 C-3)
**Grant the strong version:** Foundry/AIP is production-real at scale (FY2025 rev **$4.475B** per 10-K; NHS Federated Data Platform ~137 trusts; Tampa General 12+ AIP use cases). It genuinely provides an **operational Ontology** (objects, links, **action types**, functions), **granular/dynamic object-level security**, **scenarios/simulations**, **governed write-back**, decision intelligence, supply-chain state, and human+agent workflows. The strawman "Palantir centralizes; OMNI federates" is **false** — Palantir supports cross-org shared ontologies and multi-party workflows.

**The precise residual (not "OMNI has ontology + agents + governance" — Palantir has all three).** Palantir's cross-org model is **federation *inside one enrollment* on a shared schema with delegated write-back**: parties join a governed instance and accept a common ontology + permissioning. It does **not** solve **cross-*sovereign* coordination** where the counterparty (a) is **independently licensed**, (b) **owns its own execution truth** in its own systems, (c) **can refuse**, and (d) **has not delegated command** to the platform. The six-question residual (Knox) that survives a full Palantir steelman:
1. who has authority to **commit** what;
2. which **independent** party accepted **custody**;
3. what **patient consequence** survives a **local failure**;
4. who may **selectively reopen** which domain;
5. what must remain **portable** across authorities;
6. **why an outside institution should accept the resulting proof without surrendering its authority to the platform.**
Palantir *can technically model* much of this **for parties who join its instance**; it does not deliver *earned acceptance across sovereigns who will not*. `as_of 2026-07-22` **no capability in the Palantir map reached T6 independently-verified outcome** for such cross-sovereign healthcare custody (headline outcomes are vendor/customer-reported; at least one publicly contested).

**Critical nuance for the A2A frame (frozen-matrix claim 3):** every documented deployment **automates intake/data-entry/communication and then routes to a human pharmacist pre-check.** There is **no evidence of fully autonomous cross-organizational order *acceptance* with legal custody transfer.** The AI bridges the fax/portal gap by *impersonating a human at the pharmacy's screen* — which is itself **proof that structured cross-org acceptance APIs are absent.** The typed A2A message set OMNI theorizes maps largely onto **existing NCPDP SCRIPT taxonomy + Empower-style webhooks**; it is *profiled from the standard*, not net-new grammar.

### §3.3 — Billing + quality/custody documentation
- **Billing:** office-use/clinic-bill (503B buy-and-bill, net-30) vs patient-specific (503A, DTC overwhelmingly **cash-pay**). Cash-pay dominates DTC — a core reason the vertical exists (bypasses PBM adjudication). Relevant to §4 AKS analysis: cash-pay narrows *federal* AKS reach.
- **Quality/custody (regulatory bedrock, T6):** 503B = cGMP (21 CFR 210/211), **every-batch potency/sterility/endotoxin/particulate testing**, valid COA for bulks. 503A = USP <795>/<797> + state boards, BUD from stability data. Labeling (21 USC §353b(a)(10)): "compounded drug," lot, BUD, "Office Use Only" where non-patient-specific. Recall: notify ≤24h, affected-lot + distribution list, 21 CFR Part 7.
- **Key finding:** COA / potency / sterility / lot / recall obligations live in **law + bilateral quality agreements / BAAs — NOT exposed as structured API artifacts anywhere found.** So a "quality-evidence profile" (charter §3.1#7) has no interoperable substrate today.

### §3.4 — G2-B verdict
Prescriber→pharmacy fulfillment in the mid-2026 cash-pay/compounding DTC market is achieved **overwhelmingly via ownership + proprietary portals/fax + bilateral contracts** — **not** a neutral multi-pharmacy structured-acceptance fabric. Exactly **two** near-real structured cross-org order APIs surfaced (**Empower REST**, **LifeFile B2B**), both **single-vendor, credential-gated, one-pharmacy-at-a-time**. Contractual reliance (T5) is **bilateral**, never via an open multi-party fabric. **No T6 independently-verified multi-pharmacy structured-acceptance-with-custody fabric exists as of 2026-07-22.** A non-owned, multi-pharmacy, structured-acceptance integration would require: a structured order schema beyond free-text; per-pharmacy credentialing + NPI/EPCS; an explicit *received→validated→accepted(or refused/clarified)→in-process→shipped* state machine **with the pharmacy able to refuse**; formulary/price/availability/quote-validity feeds; status/refill/cancel + shipping callbacks; **quality/custody artifacts as first-class objects**; and a **legal layer** (services + BAA + quality/liability agreements) — i.e., acceptance is **as much contractual as technical**. None of this exists as a standard.

## §4 — G2-C · Institutional, regulatory, and economic classification (where 10BN becomes an institution or dissolves into theater)

**Rule for this stream (Knox):** what does OMNI legally/commercially *become* as it grows; who pays; what fees depend on; what creates self-preference; whether a neutral network can earn attractive economics without becoming the conflicted actor it was designed to prevent.

This is the textbook conflicted middleman. | DRC + Litigated |
| **GPO** | Buy-side procurement | **AKS GPO safe harbor (42 CFR 1001.952(j)):** vendor fee **≤3%** (or disclosed amount), **annual disclosure**, **not member-owned.** A legislated path to sell-side fees without AKS liability — bounded by cap + disclosure + no self-ownership. | DRC |
| **Marketplace / conformance-or-certification body** | Framework/trust services | **DirectTrust/EHNAC** + **TEFCA/RCE**: sustain economics on **membership + accreditation + framework services**, and **structurally prohibit transaction/exchange fees** (TEFCA bans QHIN-to-QHIN + Treatment/IAS query fees). **GoodRx Community Link (Jun 2025):** cash-pay, **fixed admin fee collected from the patient**, PBM-free — evidence a routing fee can sit on the *patient* side, not the pharmacy-referral side. | DRC/MP |

### §4.2 — The fee constraint that governs everything (AKS)
`42 USC 1320a-7b(b)` is intent-based, both-sided, "any remuneration" to induce referrals/purchasing of federally-reimbursable items. **The single structural feature most in tension with it is a per-transaction / volume-based routing fee paid by pharmacies.** The **referral-services safe harbor (42 CFR 1001.952(f))** requires fees **assessed equally, based only on operating cost, NOT on volume/value of referrals** + disclosures. HHS-OIG has issued an **unfavorable opinion on a pay-to-play referral platform** whose fees varied by geography/#locations. **FMV ≠ safe** (2026 OIG FAQ). **Cash-pay narrows federal AKS reach but does not eliminate risk** (state anti-kickback/fee-splitting + Beneficiary-Inducement CMP + federal-nexus survive). The two documented "compliant fee" templates: **patient-side fixed fee (GoodRx Community Link)** and **GPO safe harbor (≤3%, disclosed, no member-ownership)**.

### §4.3 — G2-C verdict
At scale, an OMNI-style prescription-routing network most likely functions as a **HIN** (info-blocking obligations attach *by function, regardless of cash-pay*) and operationally resembles an **e-prescribing switch**; depending on fee design it can drift toward a **referral service** (AKS) or **GPO** (procurement). It becomes a **PBM only if it manages benefits** — the category to avoid. **The failure mode that regulators/courts are actively punishing in 2026 is vertical integration + volume-linked economics + a self-owned counterparty** (PBMs, Surescripts loyalty pricing, Amazon/Google self-preferencing). This is precisely the care-commerce firewall's institutional stakes.

## §5 — The rung-5 test (Knox order item 9): can an ordinary contract + webhook + work queue reach acceptance rung 5? — and the honest narrowing of OMNI's residual

**Why this test matters:** if a plain integration reaches rung 5 ("counterparty accepts execution custody"), then OMNI's claimed R4 frontier is narrower than the charter implies, and honesty requires narrowing it.

**The strongest real-world instance is not hypothetical — it exists.** Empower Pharmacy's **REST API + webhooks (§3.1)** is essentially *an ordinary contract + webhook + work queue*: an order is POSTed; a `Received` webhook fires meaning "Empower **accepts** the order… received and **validated**"; then `Processing`; then `Complete` with shipment tracking. That is a working, single-counterparty, structured acceptance signal.

**Honest finding — graded on the two ladders:**

| Rung | Can plain contract+webhook+work-queue reach it? | Evidence / caveat |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 transport/schema/ACK | **Yes, trivially** | this *is* what a webhook + validation does |
| 4 accepted-for-review | **Yes** | Empower `Received/validated`; a work queue = "queued for the pharmacist" |
| **5 execution custody (thin/happy-path)** | **Yes, for ONE counterparty** | Empower's `accepts` semantic is a genuine thin rung-5 for that single pharmacy on the happy path. **So OMNI must NOT claim rung-5-per-counterparty as an unreached frontier.** |
| 6 consequence/exception semantics under partial failure | **No** | A status webhook says "processing"; it does **not** guarantee *no false closure when the pharmacy goes silent*, *selective reopening of the right domain*, *honest patient projection after known failure*, or *compensation ≠ rollback*. Per `D0W3B-GRD-002`: message-receipt/local-queue-acceptance **≠** patient-consequence completion. This is the EVRUN-008 **R3** residual — design-only, no incumbent provides it. |
| 7–9 contractual reliance / behavior change / repeated adoption | **No** | These require an **operated network + earned trust**, not a webhook. Empower's custody is proprietary + single-vendor; nobody contractually relies on a *portable* OMNI custody/proof across counterparties. |

**The narrowing (done honestly, per item 9).** OMNI's residual is **not** "external acceptance" writ large — that overclaims, because (a) incumbents own rungs 1–4 at T4–T5 scale, and (b) a single-vendor **thin rung-5 already exists** (Empower). The residual that survives the steelman narrows to the conjunction that **no incumbent, standard, vertical, or ordinary integration provides**:
1. **Cross-N-counterparty *portable* custody** — the same authorized clinical commitment accepted, with custody semantics, across *multiple independently-licensed pharmacies* **without re-integrating a bespoke bilateral API/contract each time** (today every real acceptance is single-vendor + bilateral: Empower, LifeFile, Hims↔partner);
2. **Rung-6 consequence-continuity under partial failure** — no false closure, selective reopening, honest projection, non-orphaning **when a rail is silent/fails** (the R3 residual; a webhook does not carry it);
3. **Admissibility-before-optimization** — the temporal, `as_of` regulatory + clinical + contractual gate applied *before* any price/speed routing, so **margin never authors the clinical recommendation** (no incumbent routing layer enforces this — PBMs do the opposite);
4. **Earned rungs 7–9 across sovereigns** — contractual reliance on, and behavior change from, an OMNI-style *portable* proof, which is an **operated-network + trust** achievement, not an architecture.

This is a real, defensible residual — **but note it is currently at the *design/conformance* tier (EVRUN-008 R3; fixture-only), not production, and R4 remains unavailable.** G2A does not change that; it sharpens *what* must be proven and confirms *no one else has proven it either*.

## §6 — Operator-neutrality as a HYPOTHESIS (Knox order item 10), not a moral declaration or a competitor impossibility

Operator-neutrality is treated here as a **testable market/governance hypothesis** requiring credible governance + market evidence — not a virtue claim, and **not** something competitors are technically incapable of.

- **Competitors *can* build firewall mechanisms.** An owned vertical (Hims-class) or a platform (Palantir-class) can implement separate clinical/commercial teams, ranking exclusions, disclosures, audit, patient choice, external referral. The firewall is a **copyable software/process rule** — it is **not** itself a moat.
- **What is hard to possess is credible neutrality *while keeping self-preference economics.*** The 2026 enforcement record is the evidence base: PBM self-preferencing (FTC reports + Express Scripts/Caremark settlements), Surescripts loyalty pricing (antitrust), Amazon/Google marketplace self-preferencing. **The failure mode is vertical integration + volume-linked fees + owned counterparty — not intermediation itself.**
- **Neutrality *is* monetizable — conditionally.** DirectTrust/EHNAC (membership + accreditation), TEFCA/RCE (fees structurally banned exactly at the conflict seam), and the GPO safe harbor (≤3%, disclosed, no member-ownership), plus GoodRx Community Link (patient-side fixed fee) are **existence proofs that a sell-side- or patient-side-funded intermediary can be lawful and durable** — *iff* the fee is **decoupled from routing volume/value and from ownership of any routed pharmacy.**
- **Net hypothesis (`[HYP]`):** *earned operator-neutrality is economically viable if and only if OMNI's fee is paid for a service/accreditation/standard/continuity function (or a capped, disclosed, patient-side fee) — NOT for the referral — and OMNI owns no preferred routed pharmacy.* The moment economics scale with *where* prescriptions route and OMNI has a preferred destination, the model converges on the PBM/self-preferencing failure regulators are punishing. **Direct empirical "trust → counterparty-behavior-change" evidence for prescription routing does not yet exist** — it remains the thing a G10 pilot + operated network must earn (rungs 7–9). The firewall is OMNI's *license to form the network*; **earned neutral acceptance is the moat — and even that is a hypothesis until counterparties behave differently because they trust it.**

## §7 — No scalar score, no universal acceptance object (Knox order item 11) — what the evidence forces

The G2A evidence **independently forces** this discipline (it is not merely a stylistic rule):
- The reality shows **different, non-commensurable evidence classes** varying by product/formulation · state · time · patient · rail · service level · contract: NCPDP conformance (T4) ≠ RxFill adoption (T2) ≠ Empower structured custody (T2, single-vendor) ≠ 503B cGMP/COA (T6, in law) ≠ DSCSA tracing (T3, upstream only) ≠ portal/fax reachability. **Collapsing these into "Pharmacy A = 94" would erase exactly the distinctions that decide admissibility and safety.**
- Likewise there is **no universal "acceptance" object**: acceptance is rung-stratified (1–10), counterparty-specific, and today mostly bilateral/single-vendor. A single `acceptance = true` boolean would hide *which rung, which counterparty, under which contract, as-of when.*
- **Therefore** the network must preserve **multidimensional, temporal, sourced evidence** (the network-scale analog of killing the scalar "best pharmacy"): admissible-set + failed/unknown constraints + stale assertions + declared objective/incentive lineage + material alternatives + the actor/policy authorized to select — never an opaque score, never a universal acceptance flag. This is consistent with C4.5 temporal discipline and frozen-matrix claim 7.

## §9 — REQUIRED FINAL QUESTION (Knox)

> **After granting incumbents their strongest actual capabilities, what remains technically unprovided, institutionally unaccepted, economically unproven, or strategically defensible for OMNI?**

Granting the strong versions — NCPDP/Surescripts own rungs 1–4 at T4–T5 (2.6B e-Rx/yr, a real change/renewal negotiation loop, RTPB); vertical DTC (Hims) makes it effortless by owning 503A+503B+peptide-mfg+labs+platform; Empower/LifeFile expose real single-vendor structured order APIs with a thin rung-5 custody signal; Palantir provides genuine cross-org ontology+actions+security+write-back; OpenAI/Anthropic provide a commoditizing, capable agent layer (MCP now Linux-Foundation-governed) — the honest remainder is:

- **Technically unprovided.** A **neutral, multi-pharmacy, structured acceptance-with-custody fabric across independently-licensed *sovereign* counterparties** — carrying **portable** custody (no bespoke bilateral re-integration per pharmacy), **rung-6 consequence-continuity under partial failure** (no false closure / selective reopening / honest projection / non-orphaning when a rail is silent), and **temporal `as_of` admissibility gating applied before optimization** so margin never authors care. Every real acceptance today is **single-vendor + bilateral** (Empower/LifeFile/Hims-partner); compounds still ride free-text/fax; RxFill closed-loop is under-adopted; quality/custody artifacts live in law + bilateral agreements, not as interoperable objects. **No standard, incumbent, or ordinary contract+webhook+work-queue provides the *cross-sovereign portable* version (§5).**
- **Institutionally unaccepted.** **R4 remains unavailable** — no external counterparty *contractually relies on* or *changes action because of* an OMNI-style portable custody/proof (rungs 7–9 unearned; the thin rung-5 that exists is proprietary/single-vendor). Palantir models cross-org only for parties who **join its enrollment on a shared schema with delegated write-back**; it does not deliver **earned acceptance across sovereigns who will not delegate command.** No T6 independently-verified cross-sovereign custody outcome exists.
- **Economically unproven.** Whether a **credibly neutral** clearing/continuity network can earn **attractive economics without becoming the conflicted actor** (PBM failure mode) is a live **`[HYP]`**: supported by DirectTrust/TEFCA/GPO/GoodRx-Community-Link templates (fees decoupled from routing volume + no owned counterparty), contradicted wherever volume-linked fees + a self-owned pharmacy appear. At scale OMNI likely **becomes a HIN** (info-blocking duties, $1M/violation, *even in cash-pay*) and resembles a **switch**; the AKS referral-services safe harbor forces **cost-based, volume-neutral, disclosed** fees. **No proof yet that the neutral model is durable and lucrative.**
- **Strategically defensible.** An **operated cross-authority conformance + routing + continuity network** for consequential care commitments: the **acceptance graph** of counterparties/relationships/conformance/reliability/quality/continuity history + **admissibility firewall as the license to form it** + **earned neutral acceptance as the moat** — expressed as **multidimensional temporal evidence, never a scalar score or universal acceptance object.** Defensible **only if earned** (great vertical product → real transactions → one painful external seam → accepted custody → trusted portable proof → repeatable participation → operated network) — **not by architecture, ontology, or agent novelty**, all of which incumbents already have or can commoditize. *Open grammar. Operated runtime. Earned acceptance network.*

**One line:** after the steelman, OMNI's remainder is **not** ontology, agents, transport, or even single-pharmacy structured acceptance (all provided or commoditizing) — it is the **cross-sovereign, portable, failure-continuous, admissibility-gated, credibly-neutral operated network**, which is **technically unbuilt, institutionally unaccepted (R4), and economically unproven** today, and therefore **strategically defensible precisely because it must be *earned in the world* rather than declared.**
