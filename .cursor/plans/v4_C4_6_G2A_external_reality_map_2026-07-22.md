### §2.1 — NCPDP SCRIPT + Surescripts (the real US prescribing rail)
The single most important steelman: **incumbent rails already provide acceptance rungs 1–4 at genuine cross-org, contractually-relied scale.** OMNI must claim zero credit for these.

| Capability | Highest honest tier | Evidence (`as_of 2026-07-22`) |
|---|---|---|

| Prescriber↔pharmacy **negotiation loop** (`RxChangeRequest/Response`: Approved / ApprovedWithChanges / Denied / Validated; `RxRenewalRequest/Response`) | **T4** cross-org | A *real structured accept / approve-with-changes / deny state exchange across org boundaries* — not just transport. | `CancelRx` / `CancelRxResponse`; `Status` / `Verify` / `Error` (ACK/receipt/fault) | **T3–T4** | Production; the ACK/receipt layer = rungs 2–3. |

| Real-Time Prescription Benefit (RTPB) | **T4**, partial **T6** | ~889,907 prescribers (2024, Surescripts); **788M responses** (2024 — *this figure not verbatim-confirmed in the release text; see Appendix A RAIL-07*); partial third-party outcome evidence (Desai et al., *JAMA Intern Med* 2022, T6). |
| Fill/dispense closed loop (`RxFill`: dispensed / partial / not-dispensed) | **T2**, patchy **T3** | The *canonical* dispense-status message — but **historically low adoption**, prescriber-opt-out, NIST-noted barriers. **This is the seam where the "closed loop" breaks in the real world.** |
| Structured & Codified Sig | **T2**, ~T3 | ~10% structured use (2020); "47% of free-text could have been structured." |
| Electronic prior authorization (ePA transaction set) | **T2** → mandate arriving | Becoming *mandatory in certified EHRs by 2027-12-31* (ONC HTI-4). |

**Version/mandate timeline (hard dated `as_of` facts — feed C4.5 temporal discipline):**
- SCRIPT **2017071** exclusive since 2020-01-01; **CMS-4205-F2** (final 2024-06-17) sets **2023011 mandatory/exclusive 2028-01-01**, 2017071 **sunsets 2027-12-31** (transition period allows either until then).
- **F&B v60** (plan-level batch formulary) + **RTPB v13** (patient-level real-time benefit) **mandatory 2027-01-01**.
- **2023011 adds a dedicated compound section, REMS elements, e-controlled-substance transfers, formalized ePA** — i.e., the standard *is* moving toward structured compounds, **but not required until 2028**.

**Steelman verdict:** the rails genuinely solve *transport + schema + ACK + accepted-for-review + a real change/renewal negotiation loop + medication history + real-time benefit* at T4–T5. What they do **not** natively carry: cross-authority *execution-custody* semantics, patient-consequence continuity under partial failure, portable admissibility, and a *structured, adopted* fill/dispense closed loop (RxFill is T2/patchy-T3). Rungs 1–4 = incumbent. Rung 5+ = open.

### §2.5 — Palantir Foundry + AIP (accurate steelman — Knox Patch 9 / §12 C-3)
**Grant the strong version:** Foundry/AIP is production-real at scale (FY2025 rev **$4.475B** per 10-K; NHS Federated Data Platform ~137 trusts; Tampa General 12+ AIP use cases). It genuinely provides an **operational Ontology** (objects, links, **action types**, functions), **granular/dynamic object-level security**, **scenarios/simulations**, **governed write-back**, decision intelligence, supply-chain state, and human+agent workflows. The strawman "Palantir centralizes; OMNI federates" is **false** — Palantir supports cross-org shared ontologies and multi-party workflows.

**The precise residual (not "OMNI has ontology + agents + governance" — Palantir has all three).** Palantir's cross-org model is **federation *inside one enrollment* on a shared schema with delegated write-back**: parties join a governed instance and accept a common ontology + permissioning. It does **not** solve **cross-*sovereign* coordination** where the counterparty (a) is **independently licensed**, (b) **owns its own execution truth** in its own systems, (c) **can refuse**, and (d) **has not delegated command** to the platform. The six-question residual (Knox) that survives a full Palantir steelman:
1. who has authority to **commit** what;
2. which **independent** party accepted **custody**;
3. what **patient consequence** survives a **local failure**;
4. who may **selectively reopen** which domain;
5. what must remain **portable** across authorities;
6. **why an outside institution should accept the resulting proof without surrendering its authority to the platform.**
Palantir *can technically model* much of this **for parties who join its instance**; it does not deliver *earned acceptance across sovereigns who will not*. `as_of 2026-07-22` **no capability in the Palantir map reached T6 independently-verified outcome** for such cross-sovereign healthcare custody (headline outcomes are vendor/customer-reported; at least one publicly contested).

### §4.2 — The fee constraint that governs everything (AKS)
`42 USC 1320a-7b(b)` is intent-based, both-sided, "any remuneration" to induce referrals/purchasing of federally-reimbursable items. **The single structural feature most in tension with it is a per-transaction / volume-based routing fee paid by pharmacies.** The **referral-services safe harbor (42 CFR 1001.952(f))** requires fees **assessed equally, based only on operating cost, NOT on volume/value of referrals** + disclosures. HHS-OIG has issued an **unfavorable opinion on a pay-to-play referral platform** whose fees varied by geography/#locations (OIG AO 26-15; contrast the favorable AO 23-04). **The "FMV alone is not a safe harbor" principle appears in OIG advisory opinions — the previously-cited "2026 OIG FAQ" could NOT be located and is withdrawn as a citation** (see Appendix A INST-07). **Cash-pay narrows federal AKS reach but does not eliminate risk** (state anti-kickback/fee-splitting + Beneficiary-Inducement CMP + federal-nexus survive). Two documented constrained-monetization *analogies*: **patient-side fixed fee (GoodRx Community Link)** and **GPO safe harbor (≤3%, disclosed, no member-ownership)**.

> **Analogies ≠ pre-cleared solution (Knox [C4]).** TEFCA, GPOs, accreditation bodies, GoodRx-style patient-side fees, and the referral-services safe harbor are **not interchangeable legal templates.** They demonstrate that **neutral infrastructure *can* be monetized under constrained arrangements** — they do **not** establish a safe OMNI revenue model. No safe-harbor conclusion may be drawn without counsel and actual transaction facts. **OMNI's economic design remains a G3/G8/G10 question + qualified legal review**, not a G2 conclusion.

### §4.3 — G2-C verdict
At scale, an OMNI-style prescription-routing network **may satisfy the functional HIN/HIE definition** (a material legal-design risk requiring qualified legal review, **not a concluded identity** — [C3]) and operationally resembles an **e-prescribing switch**; depending on fee design it could implicate the **referral-services safe harbor** (AKS) or **GPO** rules (procurement). It becomes a **PBM only if it manages benefits** — the category to avoid. Fee-model precedents (TEFCA/GPO/GoodRx/referral safe harbor) show constrained monetization is *possible* but are **not interchangeable and do not pre-clear OMNI's model** ([C4]); the economic design is a **G3/G8/G10 + counsel** question. **The failure mode that regulators/courts are actively punishing in 2026 is vertical integration + volume-linked economics + a self-owned counterparty** (PBMs, Surescripts loyalty pricing, Amazon/Google self-preferencing). This is precisely the care-commerce firewall's institutional stakes.

## §5 — The rung-5 test (Knox order item 9): can an ordinary contract + webhook + work queue reach acceptance rung 5? — and the honest narrowing of OMNI's residual

**Why this test matters:** if a plain integration reaches rung 5 ("counterparty accepts execution custody"), then OMNI's claimed R4 frontier is narrower than the charter implies, and honesty requires narrowing it.

**The strongest real-world instance is not hypothetical — it exists, but it does NOT reach rung 5 on the documentation alone ([C2]).** Empower Pharmacy's **REST API + webhooks (§3.1)** is essentially *an ordinary contract + webhook + work queue*: an order is POSTed; a `Received` webhook fires meaning the system *received + validated* the order; then `Processing` (pharmacy staff began work / entered fulfillment workflow); then `Complete` with shipment tracking. That is a working, single-counterparty, structured **validation + workflow** signal — **it is not published evidence that the pharmacy contractually assumed execution custody.**

**Honest finding — graded on the two ladders (corrected per Knox [C2]):**

| Evidence | Honest rung | Why |
|---|---|---|
| transport / schema / ACK | **1–3** | this *is* what a webhook + validation does |
| **API `Received` (received + system-validated)** | **3–4** | proves the API accepted + validated the submission — **not** custody |
| **Pharmacy `Processing` (staff began work / entered fulfillment workflow)** | **4 (provisional 4.5)** | local-queue work-start; per `D0W3B-GRD-002`, local-queue acceptance **≠** cross-authority custody |
| **Contractually-defined assumption of an execution obligation** (refusal, exception, deadline, non-orphaning, termination, liability/remedy semantics) | **5** | **requires a contract, not an API event.** A one-pharmacy *contract* might reach rung 5; **the public API documentation alone does not prove it.** |
| Accepted exception / non-orphaning semantics under partial failure | **6** | no false closure when the pharmacy goes silent, selective reopening of the right domain, honest patient projection after known failure, compensation ≠ rollback. EVRUN-008 **R3** residual — design-only; **no inspected public primary source established an incumbent providing it.** |
| contractual reliance / behavior change / repeated adoption | **7–9** | require an **operated network + earned trust**, not a webhook; no evidence anyone contractually relies on a *portable* cross-counterparty custody/proof. |

**Does a plain contract + webhook + work-queue reach rung 5?** Rungs 1–4 (transport→schema→ACK→accepted-for-review): **yes, trivially** — Empower demonstrates it. Rung 5 (contractual execution custody): **only if the contract itself carries the execution-obligation semantics** (refusal/exception/deadline/non-orphaning/termination) — the *webhook does not*. So the corrected implication for OMNI: **the first external proof (a G10 pilot) must include contractual + operational acceptance semantics, not merely a better webhook.** This does **not** weaken the 10BN thesis; it clarifies the *actual* pilot requirement.

**The narrowing (done honestly, per item 9).** OMNI's residual is **not** "external acceptance" writ large — that overclaims, because incumbents own rungs 1–4 at T4–T5 scale and a single-vendor structured **validation/workflow** signal (rung 3–4/4.5) already exists (Empower). But the residual is **not** eliminated at rung 5 either: no inspected public source shows the *contractual* rung-5 semantics reached in a portable, multi-counterparty way. The residual that survives the steelman narrows to the conjunction that **no inspected public primary source established any incumbent, standard, vertical, or ordinary integration providing**:
1. **Cross-N-counterparty *portable* custody** — the same authorized clinical commitment accepted, **with contractual execution-obligation semantics (rung 5, not a webhook)**, across *multiple independently-licensed pharmacies* **without re-integrating a bespoke bilateral API/contract each time** (in inspected sources every real structured acceptance is single-vendor + bilateral: Empower, LifeFile, Hims↔partner);
2. **Rung-6 consequence-continuity under partial failure** — no false closure, selective reopening, honest projection, non-orphaning **when a rail is silent/fails** (the R3 residual; a webhook does not carry it);
3. **Admissibility-before-optimization** — the temporal, `as_of` regulatory + clinical + contractual gate applied *before* any price/speed routing, so **margin never authors the clinical recommendation** (no incumbent routing layer enforces this — PBMs do the opposite);
4. **Earned rungs 7–9 across sovereigns** — contractual reliance on, and behavior change from, an OMNI-style *portable* proof, which is an **operated-network + trust** achievement, not an architecture.

This is a real, defensible residual — **but note it is currently at the *design/conformance* tier (EVRUN-008 R3; fixture-only), not production, and R4 remains unavailable.** G2A does not change that; it sharpens *what* must be proven and confirms *no one else has proven it either*.

## §6 — Operator-neutrality as a HYPOTHESIS (Knox order item 10), not a moral declaration or a competitor impossibility

Operator-neutrality is treated here as a **testable market/governance hypothesis** requiring credible governance + market evidence — not a virtue claim, and **not** something competitors are technically incapable of.

- **Competitors *can* build firewall mechanisms.** An owned vertical (Hims-class) or a platform (Palantir-class) can implement separate clinical/commercial teams, ranking exclusions, disclosures, audit, patient choice, external referral. The firewall is a **copyable software/process rule** — it is **not** itself a moat.
- **What is hard to possess is credible neutrality *while keeping self-preference economics.*** The 2026 enforcement record is the evidence base: PBM self-preferencing (FTC reports + Express Scripts/Caremark settlements), Surescripts loyalty pricing (antitrust), Amazon/Google marketplace self-preferencing. **The failure mode is vertical integration + volume-linked fees + owned counterparty — not intermediation itself.**
- **Neutrality *may* be monetizable under constrained arrangements — these are analogies, not a pre-cleared model ([C4]).** DirectTrust/EHNAC (membership + accreditation), TEFCA/RCE (fees structurally banned exactly at the conflict seam), the GPO safe harbor (≤3%, disclosed, no member-ownership), and GoodRx Community Link (patient-side fixed fee) show that a sell-side- or patient-side-funded intermediary **can exist under constrained conditions** — *apparently iff* the fee is **decoupled from routing volume/value and from ownership of any routed pharmacy.** They are **not interchangeable templates** and **do not establish a safe OMNI revenue model**; that requires counsel + actual transaction facts (a G3/G8/G10 question).
- **Net hypothesis (`[HYP]`):** *earned operator-neutrality is economically viable if and only if OMNI's fee is paid for a service/accreditation/standard/continuity function (or a capped, disclosed, patient-side fee) — NOT for the referral — and OMNI owns no preferred routed pharmacy.* The moment economics scale with *where* prescriptions route and OMNI has a preferred destination, the model converges on the PBM/self-preferencing failure regulators are punishing. **Direct empirical "trust → counterparty-behavior-change" evidence for prescription routing does not yet exist** — it remains the thing a G10 pilot + operated network must earn (rungs 7–9). The firewall is OMNI's *license to form the network*; **earned neutral acceptance is the moat — and even that is a hypothesis until counterparties behave differently because they trust it.**

## §7 — No scalar score, no universal acceptance object (Knox order item 11) — what the evidence forces

This is consistent with C4.5 temporal discipline and frozen-matrix claim 7.

Operated runtime. Earned acceptance network.*

**One line:** after the steelman, OMNI's remainder is **not** ontology, agents, transport, or even single-pharmacy structured acceptance (all provided or commoditizing) — it is the **cross-sovereign, portable, failure-continuous, admissibility-gated, credibly-neutral operated network**, which is **technically unbuilt, institutionally unaccepted (R4), and economically unproven** today, and therefore **strategically defensible precisely because it must be *earned in the world* rather than declared.**
