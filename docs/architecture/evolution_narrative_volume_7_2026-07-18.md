## What the incumbent steelman taught
The blind Gemini submission composed Epic + ServiceNow + Microsoft/Nuance + Stripe + Surescripts and honestly showed that mature incumbents already provide substantial identity, clinical authority, state, workflow, SLAs/timers, e-prescribing transport, refunds, portal status, local audit, and manual recovery. But it also showed (and admitted) that the composition **does not natively provide the complete cross-authority continuity function** without custom semantics and a **shadow coordination state** (ServiceNow becoming the cross-domain "case"), and it flattened several exact state semantics (FHIR resource statuses, Stripe PaymentIntent-vs-Refund, AuditEvent-vs-Provenance). Its sharpest contribution was operating realism: a governed path that is slower loses to personal texting + ChatGPT — the primary product falsifier.
