- **C3.5:** the fear was that "hospital-grade" would force either false-confidence ("we replace Epic now") or false-retreat ("we just coordinate EHRs forever"). The arc was designed so both fail — market-posture (GTM) was separated from architecture-obligation (substrate physics).

The real question was **clinical-trial substrate readiness**. The arc was reframed (G4 §0.5) without redoing the work.
