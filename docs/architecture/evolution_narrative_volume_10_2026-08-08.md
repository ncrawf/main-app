## §1 — What the arc was asked, and what it answered

Insurance began as one commissioned Phase-A object: a Gate-0 carrier answering whether mixed financing needs its own domain. It ended three gates later with a result that is more useful than either yes or no.

**The question was malformed, and the arc's first real contribution was noticing.** "Does Insurance need a domain?" conflates two independent questions — *who owns the truth* and *what must OMNI assemble*. Gate 1b split them:

> **Axis 1 — `NO_SHARED_FINANCING_TRUTH_SUBSTRATE`.** No payer-named domain, no universal financing truth owner. Truths are source-native, exchanged through the ratified generic exchange.
>
> **Axis 2 — `FIRST_CLASS_MIXED_FINANCING_OPERATING_PROFILE REQUIRED`.** OMNI still needs a coherent operating assembly over those distributed truths. It owns no source truth and is still first-class product architecture.

The failure mode this exists to prevent is precise: reading *"Insurance is not a domain"* as *"Insurance does not deserve a first-class operating assembly."* That would leave OMNI with correct physics and no product. The distinctions are **no domain ≠ no product · no truth owner ≠ no assessment lifecycle · no universal position lifecycle ≠ no coherent end-to-end assembly.**

It also refuses two named losing moves: the **Epic-like collapse**, where financing facts silently distort care truth; and the **god-ontology collapse**, where a central financing object becomes the owner of everyone else's truth.

## §2 — The recurring defect: correction without propagation

Gate 1b took nine revisions. Its own §0.5 names why, and the pattern is the durable lesson.

**R5 through R9's dominant failure was not wrong architecture. It was wrong propagation** — a claim corrected in the section that introduced it and left live in a receipt, an exception row, a Gate-2 output, or a source posture. The structural cause: the carrier **restated its own state in roughly eight places**, which is exactly the single-source violation it invoked against other artifacts.

Three instances are worth preserving because each shows a different detection failure:

- **C56** — a reviewer correction was rejected on the basis of a presence-only `grep` that returned 1. The grep could not distinguish *"present in the file"* from *"present in the correct section,"* and the result was used to overrule a reviewer who was right.
- **The R9 self-reference** — the pass whose entire purpose was ending propagation defects appended `R9 (this)` without normalizing `R8 (this)`, producing instance nine.
- **A case-sensitive sweep** — a later "clean sweep" missed a live contradiction because it searched `remains ACTIVE` while the text said `remains active`.

**Every one of these was a proof method failing, not an architecture failing.** That is why the parent carry's `E1` obligation is a narrow mechanical proof against assembled bytes rather than another reading of prose, and why the preservation packet ships its own digest utility rather than trusting each future agent to re-derive a convention.

## §3 — The preservation failure, caught by the operator

Three subagents were commissioned at Gate-0 kickoff: a **blind architecture decomposer**, an **anti-OMNI adversary**, and a **patient + provider lens**. Their conclusions were adopted — the decomposer became the carrier's spine, the adversary **changed a verdict**, the patient/provider lens produced the conflict analysis the carrier refused to average.

Their raw submissions existed nowhere in the repository. Months later they survived only because the operator still held them locally.

**The estate had already written the rule.** The Agent Runtime & Harness capture §6 required that a child *"returns a structured result + evidence pointers, NOT its whole transcript."* But `agent_work_protocol.md` — the mandatory loop every agent runs — contained **zero occurrences** of `subagent`, `sub-agent` or `delegat`. The contract sat where nobody must read it; the document everybody must read never asked the question. **A missing wire, not amnesia.** And §6 required a pointer while assigning nobody ownership of creating the object it points at.

~267 KB across six verbatim regions is now preserved with canonical digests, an index that routes by question, and named consumers. Two things in those raws could not have been recovered from the carrier: the **decomposer's reasoning behind a model that was later demoted at 0R** — the carrier records that the conclusion died, nothing records why it was reached — and the **patient/provider lens self-flagging figures it could not verify from its training data.** A conclusions-only carrier has nowhere to put a rejected branch or an author's own uncertainty.

**Timeless lesson, extracted to `06` this checkpoint:** multiple live status surfaces inside one artifact drift exactly like duplicated cross-artifact state; **one current-state surface per artifact**; a stale status summary must be invalidated when the owning statement changes. The broader *"a semantic correction is incomplete until every dependent restatement is propagated or invalidated"* is **deliberately parked** with a promotion trigger — beyond-packet evidence proves duplicated state, not yet the fully generalized claim.

## §4 — What the arc learned about its own scope discipline

Two structural errors were made and corrected during the carry, both worth recording because both were **the estate's own rules being violated by the people enforcing them.**

**A hidden portfolio replan.** A revision held the Phase-A partition open through Gate 2. **A governance change bundled into a domain carry.** A global Tier-4 amendment to the mandatory protocol was pulled into the Insurance parent purely because it arose in the same conversation — taking the transaction from 10 shared surfaces to 13. **AWP §2.1 says package membership follows a shared intended outcome and coordinated closure, not co-occurrence.** Volume 9 established that discipline; this arc violated it within days and had to unwind it. The governance work is parked as its own transaction, with a follow-up pointer in the handoff so it cannot disappear.

The general form: **the estate knows things nobody is routed to.** Every significant correction in this arc came from reading a primary source that had been sitting there — the lane card, the Gate-0 sequencing sentence, `DEC-039`'s explicit permission for Task-D to consume candidate inputs, the runtime capture's own `agent_runtime_formulation_OPEN` status. None of it was new. All of it was unrouted.
