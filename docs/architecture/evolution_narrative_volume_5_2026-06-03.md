## Addendum 2026-06-03 (same arc) — the enterprise-posture resolution (c)

The AI-frame turn provoked a deeper fork (Nick + Knox): is OMNI fundamentally **(a)** a care substrate that exposes interoperability, or **(b)** a trust network that operates care? Two pressure tests resolved it as **(c)**: trust/authority/permeability is a **first-class cross-cutting AXIS** (peer to the AI axis), NOT a domain. The decisive argument was internal, not from the AI corpus — the RBAC contract §5 **four-way authority composition** (Identity/Federation/RBAC/D7/CNS-Meta/domain + AI#12 + `T0-13`) proves trust was *already decomposed*, so a "universal trust topology" domain would violate OMNI's own rules — Federation literally cannot become the company (`D0THES-GRD-031`). Two more durable findings: **trust is relocated, not eliminated** (the v39 VPN nuance — every outward rail is a new trust concentrator; govern metadata, not just payload; `D0THES-GRD-030`), and **architectural ≠ economic center** (OMNI Direct is architecturally one rail but economically NOT demoted; `D0THES-GRD-032`). Ratified as `D0THES-DEC-035` (posture `omni_enterprise_posture_2026-06-03.md`); supersedes the inward/fortress reading (`D0THES-CNF-014`). Knox's binding instruction for the next phase: author thesis v3 as a **re-grounding with explicit v2→v3 diffs**, not a rewrite. The answer got *more constrained, not less* — the sign of finding the architecture rather than inventing it.
